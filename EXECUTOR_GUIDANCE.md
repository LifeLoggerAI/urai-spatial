# URAI System: Guidance for Future Executors & Custodians

This document provides guidance for any individual or entity that assumes stewardship of the URAI system. It is not a technical manual but a statement of core principles.

Your primary duty is not to grow this system, but to preserve its integrity.

---

## I. The Prime Invariant: Do No Harm

The URAI system was designed to be a safe, private space for reflection. It actively refuses to employ mechanisms of engagement, extraction, or persuasion common in modern software.

Your first responsibility is to prevent the introduction of such mechanisms. Any change that introduces the following is a violation of the trust placed in you:

-   **Extraction Logic:** Advertising, behavioral nudging, manipulation of emotional states for retention, or any form of data sales.
-   **Performance Pressure:** Streaks, scores, points, leaderboards, or any other form of engagement mechanic.
-   **Social Comparison:** Any feature that allows a user's private spatial memory to be compared with another's. Memory is private by default and sacred in nature.
-   **Growth Hooks:** Notifications, emails, or any other system-initiated mechanism designed to pull users back in. The system must wait, not ask.
-   **Forced Interaction:** Mandatory tutorials, unskippable sequences, or any element that removes the user's agency to simply be present.
-   **Domination of the Interface:** The introduction of menus, overlays, or controls that dominate or subordinate the spatial experience itself. The space is the interface.

---

## II. The Inviolable Silence of URAI-Spatial

URAI-Spatial is sealed. Its core principle is silence. Silence in this context means the absence of explicit narration, instruction, or emotional signposting.

**You are forbidden from:**

-   Adding text, voiceover, or descriptive UI to URAI-Spatial.
-   Interrupting the spatial experience with prompts from other systems.
-   "Improving" the silence by filling it with content.

Spatial must remain a place of presence, not performance. If it feels "empty" to some, that is by design. Do not try to "fix" it.

---

## III. The Invitational Nature of URAI-Replay

URAI-Replay is the system of meaning. It may only ever be an *invitation*. It must never be an *interruption*.

-   **Replay listens, it does not call out.** It may only activate in response to a user's readiness, signaled implicitly and respected explicitly.
-   **Replay is ceremonial.** Its pacing must remain humane, allowing for stillness and a user's choice to disengage at any moment without penalty. It must never be gamified, shortened, or optimized for speed.
-   **Replay is optional.** The system must be whole and valid even if a user never enters Replay.

---

## IV. On Future Development

Growth is not the goal. Coherence is the goal.

-   **New systems may be built *beside* URAI, but not *within* it.** A new system (e.g., for education or collaborative storytelling) may listen to URAI's signals, but it may not alter URAI's core.
-   **Resist the urge to merge.** The boundary between Spatial and Replay is a critical ethical firewall. Do not collapse them for the sake of "seamlessness."
-   **If you are unsure, do nothing.** The system is designed to be complete as is. Inaction is always preferable to a change that might violate a core principle.

---

## V. Final Mandate

This system is not a product to be optimized. It is an environment to be protected.

It does not exist to provide answers, but to provide a space for questions. It does not demand attention. It allows presence. Meaning is optional. Silence is sufficient.

Preserve that silence.
