# URAI V1 - Parent Dashboard Specification

## I. Core Philosophy: Transparency, Not Surveillance

The Parent Dashboard is designed to build trust by providing transparency and control, not to act as a surveillance tool. The language and features must empower the parent to understand and guide their child's journey through URAI, without making the child feel monitored. The core principle is "conversation starter, not report card."

---

## II. Dashboard Components

The dashboard will be simple, with a focus on qualitative reflection over quantitative metrics.

### 1. Adventure Log

*   **What it is:** A simple, reverse-chronological list of the "Adventure Modules" the child has completed.
*   **What it shows:** 
    *   The name of the module (e.g., "The Whispering Woods & The Frightened Fox").
    *   The date of completion.
    *   A brief, non-clinical summary of the implicit skill explored (e.g., "This story helped explore the feeling of fear and how naming it can make it feel smaller.").
*   **What it does NOT show:**
    *   Time spent on the module.
    *   "Performance" metrics or scores.
    *   A history of attempts or failures.

### 2. The Story Box

*   **What it is:** A gallery of the creative artifacts the child has made.
*   **What it shows:**
    *   A view of the drawings, named sounds, and rhythm stars the child created in the modules.
    *   This allows the parent to see their child's creative expression directly.
*   **Purpose:** To provide a natural and positive starting point for conversation. Instead of saying, "I saw you learned about frustration today," the parent can say, "I love this drawing of the 'Silly Whisper-Wind' you made! Can you tell me about it?"

---

## III. Parent Controls: Safety & Boundaries

The controls are designed to be simple and focused on establishing healthy boundaries.

### 1. Session Limiter

*   **Functionality:** Allows the parent to set a maximum daily session time for the app.
*   **Options:** Simple presets (e.g., 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 45 minutes).
*   **Default:** 30 minutes.
*   **In-App Communication:** When time is nearly up, the Companion will provide a gentle, non-stressful wrap-up prompt (e.g., "It's almost time to say goodbye for today. Let's get our friend to a safe resting place."). The session ends calmly; it does not shut off abruptly.

### 2. "Quiet Time" Scheduler

*   **Functionality:** Allows the parent to define periods when the app is unavailable (e.g., during school hours, after 8 PM).
*   **Purpose:** To reinforce digital wellness and prevent the app from becoming an all-hours escape. It helps position the app as a specific tool for a specific time, not a constant companion.

---

## IV. Ethical Constraints & "What We Don't Do"

This section may be explicitly included in the UI to build trust.

*   **No Behavioral Reports:** This dashboard will never show analytics on your child's behavior, mood trends, or emotional state. URAI is a reflective tool, not a monitoring tool.
*   **No Comparison:** The app will never compare a child's progress or creativity to that of other children.
*   **Data Privacy:** All creative artifacts and progress logs are stored locally on your device or in your private, secure cloud account. We do not have access to this data.
