## Acceptance rules

A realm or governance lane is complete only when its exact tested SHA has retained proof appropriate to its scope. Spatial surfaces require desktop/mobile interaction, departure/arrival, navigation restoration, loading, empty/partial/error states, reduced motion, safe tier, no-WebGL fallback, keyboard equivalence, accessible semantics, console/request diagnostics, and exact build identity.

Governance surfaces require machine-verifiable default-deny controls, truthful dependency SHAs, prohibited-claim enforcement, external-authority boundaries, and no production/provider/legal mutation.

Claims such as `AAA+++`, `production ready`, `fully accessible`, `HIPAA compliant`, `patent pending`, `partnered`, or `complete` remain prohibited unless retained scope-specific evidence supports them.
