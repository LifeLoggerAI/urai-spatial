## Status vocabulary

- **VERIFIED LIVE** — externally observed on the intended deployed environment with receipt evidence.
- **VERIFIED IN REPOSITORY** — source and contract inspected; no deployment claim.
- **IMPLEMENTED BUT NOT DEPLOYED** — source repair exists but live environment is stale or unknown.
- **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED** — meaningful source exists but required behavior/evidence is incomplete.
- **BLOCKED** — cannot complete without a true external authority, credential, payment, device, or prerequisite.
- **ROADMAP** — capability milestone not yet implemented/certified.
- **REJECTED OR OBSOLETE** — superseded source or authority that must not compete with canon.
