# URAI Ground — Definitive Canon V1

Status: founder-locked product authority for Ground convergence. This document does not certify runtime pixels or release readiness by itself. `URAI_PERSONALIZED_LIVED_WORLD_AUTHORITY_V1.md` is the controlling authority for personalized terrestrial reconstruction, provenance, fidelity, person-presence, consent and global-field boundaries.

## Realm ownership

- Home is a cinematic lived-world presentation with a visible user Avatar, a separate physical Orb, broad Sky ownership for Life Map, and physical terrain ownership for Ground.
- Ground is ordinary first-person desktop/mobile exploration. It does not render a third-person Avatar, permanent hands/arms/feet, a weapon reticle, synthetic head bob, sprint/jump/crouch shooter vocabulary, or a follower Orb.
- XR is a separate embodiment mode and may use tracked hands/controllers when the hardware legitimately provides them.

## Primary lived-world authority

Ground is the user's terrestrial lived world. When authorized source material exists, Ground reconstructs the user's actual places and physical life: homes, workplaces, buildings, rooms, vehicles, roads, routes, recurring destinations, person-presences, physical objects, environmental conditions and location-bound memories.

Temperate, woodland, urban, arid and coastal families are fallback rendering grammars. They must not replace source-backed personal reality. Generic fallback is explicitly non-autobiographical and must never be presented as a remembered place.

Every personalized element carries source provenance, transformation history, confidence, privacy/consent authority, reconstruction fidelity (`confirmed`, `partial`, `unknown`), visibility and correction revision. Confirmed reconstruction requires source evidence. Unknown remains unknown. Partial reconstruction remains visibly bounded rather than silently filled with invented detail.

Ground topology may bend between places according to time, recurrence, relationship, memory and autobiographical importance, while the internal representation of a real place remains faithful to authorized evidence.

People are PersonPresences/memory-presences, not autonomous NPCs. Likeness, voice, third-party, minor/dependent, grief/legacy and biometric boundaries apply before recognizable reconstruction. New autonomous dialogue on behalf of a reconstructed person is forbidden by default.

## Entry

Pointer/touch Ground entry belongs to the visible Home terrain. There is no localized Ground portal, white dot, glowing doorway, ring, or level-select object. Drag remains look; a clean terrain selection begins the transition. A semantic Enter Ground action remains available for keyboard and assistive navigation without overlaying the ordinary world interaction.

## Embodiment and descent target

The locked authored sequence is:

1. Home terrain acknowledges the selected point.
2. The visible Avatar gives a restrained awareness response.
3. The Home camera advances toward the Avatar head/shoulder line.
4. Near-camera Avatar geometry is safely removed before clipping.
5. The user receives a brief Avatar-eye Home viewpoint with no hands.
6. The camera approaches the selected physical terrain.
7. The surface reveals material depth: vegetation, organic debris, roots, mineral, stone, dark soil and moisture where appropriate.
8. The apparent descent performs a perceptually stable spatial fold from geological depth into forward environmental depth.
9. Ground reveals as an expansive physical world with sky, terrain, atmosphere and meaningful geography.
10. Control hands off at approximately 1.69 m eye height without a camera snap.

Normal target duration is approximately 2.65 seconds. Reduced-motion target is approximately 0.45–0.60 seconds and preserves semantic continuity without rapid translation, rush, blur or aggressive parallax.

## Ground meaning

Ground is the terrestrial lived layer of a person's life: place, movement, environment, repeated routines, physical context, sensory patterns, emotional weather, memories tied to locations and lived geography.

Life Map is the far/cosmic view. Ground is the human-height physical view. Focus examines a selected memory. Replay reconstructs temporal experience. Home is the stable place from which these realms become reachable.

## Memory language

Ground memories are environmentally anchored. They are not Life Map stars, collectible pickups, floating cards, generic holograms or glowing markers. Significance increases through local atmosphere, physically situated fragments, sound, light, material traces, scene reconstruction and deliberate interaction. Private place/memory geometry mounts only when consent, source provenance, data availability and current privacy state permit a truthful representation.

A Ground-origin memory handoff preserves place, memory, era, involved person-presences, source IDs, privacy purposes, reconstruction fidelity, camera origin and semantic return token through Focus/Replay. If Ground was the actual origin, semantic unwind is Replay -> Focus -> Ground.

## Environmental families

If C4 is not granted, sensitive emotional atmosphere is suppressed without disabling ordinary physical weather.
