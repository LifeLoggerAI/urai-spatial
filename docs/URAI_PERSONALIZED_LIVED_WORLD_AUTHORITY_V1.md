# URAI Personalized Lived World Authority V1

Status: founder-locked product and architecture authority for the personalized terrestrial layer. This document defines product truth; it does not by itself certify pixels, provider behavior, privacy approval, release readiness, or deployment.

## Core authority

Ground is the user's terrestrial lived world. When authorized source material exists, Ground reconstructs the user's actual places and physical life: homes, workplaces, buildings, rooms, vehicles, roads, routes, recurring destinations, person-presences, physical objects, environmental conditions, and location-bound memories. Temperate, woodland, urban, arid, and coastal grammars are fallback rendering systems, not substitutes for available user-specific reality.

Generated detail must never be presented as observed autobiographical fact. Every personalized element binds source provenance, transformation history, confidence, current privacy/consent authority, reconstruction fidelity, visibility, and user-correction revision.

Ground topology may bend according to time, recurrence, relationship, memory and autobiographical importance. A childhood home may become adjacent to a current home or old workplace. The representation inside a real reconstructed place remains faithful to authorized evidence.

## Fidelity

- `confirmed`: source-backed and permitted. A confirmed reconstruction cannot exist without source records.
- `partial`: some characteristics are known. Unknown portions remain visibly bounded, softened, occluded, low-detail or otherwise non-assertive.
- `unknown`: no autobiographical claim is made. Generic continuation may preserve navigation only when it is unmistakably non-personal.

The user's correction overrides model inference. Corrections are versioned provenance events, not silent rewrites.

## Lived World Graph

The authoritative graph supports User, Place, Building, Room, VehiclePlace, Route, Object, PersonPresence, Memory, Event, Era, Environment and EmotionalAssociation entities. Edges express containment, location, travel, association, memory, participation, era and autobiographical adjacency.

Queries must be able to resolve source-backed places, room fidelity, vehicle ownership/context, associated people, place-bound memories, source evidence, confidence, privacy state and what must disappear or degrade after consent revocation.

## PersonPresence

People are memory-presences, not autonomous NPCs. Rendering modes are recognizable, partial, silhouette, voice-only, environmental trace, memory-specific or unavailable. Likeness, voice, third-party, minor/dependent, grief/legacy and biometric authority apply independently. New AI-generated dialogue on behalf of a reconstructed person is forbidden by default. One user's memory ownership does not grant unrestricted rights over another person's identity.

## Privacy binding

Current URAI Privacy tiers remain authoritative:

- C1 personal memory storage;
- C3 location context;
- C4 sensitive AI inference;
- C5 biometric identity;
- C6 long-term personalization;
- C7 export;
- C8 cohort-level de-identified participation.

Revocation stops future dependent processing immediately and degrades or unmounts dependent personalized world elements. Demo data is never substituted for unavailable private data.

## Personal emotional weather

Personal Emotional Weather is private sensitive inference, not objective diagnosis. It requires C4 and uses uncertainty-aware language such as possible signal, pattern, pressure, calm, mist, turbulence, recovery and clearing. Ordinary environmental weather remains a separate physical system.

## Global Emotional Field

Global Emotional Field is a cohort-level public-good aggregate, not the user's mood and never an individual inspection surface. The privacy standard's current default floor is 100 users. Location, mental-health/crisis, biometric-derived, relationship/social-graph, minors and protected-population signals require an explicitly approved higher threshold; the renderer must suppress a cell rather than invent that threshold. No raw voice, transcript, image, biometric template, movement trail, exact user location, named relationship or identifiable social graph may appear.

Insufficient safe signal renders no emotional overlay, not a false zero-emotion state.

## Ground memory semantics

Ground means where life happened. Focus means which memory/event/person/moment is being examined. Replay means what happened over time. A Ground-origin memory context carries place ID, memory ID, era, involved person-presences, source IDs, privacy purposes, reconstruction fidelity, camera origin and return token into Focus/Replay. Confirmed memory context cannot exist without source evidence.

Semantic unwind preserves origin: Replay -> Focus -> Ground when Ground was the actual origin. It must not reset generically to Life Map or Home.

## Home ownership systems

First-person Home remains camera-only and persistent. Passport is the ownership/authority surface and reuses the operational Passport backend for identity, sources, devices, consent, provenance, export, deletion, audit and recovery. It does not create a parallel consent store. Global Emotional Field is a separate physical Home system. Both must remain sparse architectural presences rather than dashboard widgets.

## Ground descent

The authored target remains approximately 2.65 seconds normally and 0.45-0.60 seconds reduced motion: physical terrain acknowledgement, Avatar awareness, camera-to-eye transfer, no hands/body, material-scale approach through vegetation/debris/root/mineral/soil/moisture detail, stable spatial fold from downward depth into forward lived-world depth, continuous audio/light transition, and handoff near 1.69 m eye height without snap, tunnel, portal, radial aperture or fade-to-black substitution.

## Visual truth

Real source authority wins over symbolism. A known house is represented as the house, not a glowing house icon. A known vehicle is represented as the vehicle, not an orb. Unknown reality may be abstracted only when the abstraction cannot be mistaken for observed memory. Production pixels reject primitive trees, box-city final art, flat blue water, obvious tiling, cheap fog walls, repeated scatter, giant bloom, particle soup, portal language and generic sci-fi signage.

## Council

Council intelligence may contextualize real life but does not replace it with fantasy architecture. Guardian can surface around privacy-sensitive context, Archivist around old artifacts/places, Builder around work/project context, Strategist around plans/routes and Witness around reflective contexts. The lived world remains primary.

## Evidence boundary

Canon is not source implementation. Source implementation is not machine verification. Machine verification is not visual acceptance. Visual acceptance is not accessibility/performance/privacy/governance approval. None of those alone constitutes independent review, Gold Master, deployment or live certification.
