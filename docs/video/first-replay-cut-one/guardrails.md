# URAI First Replay Film — Guardrails

Cut One should stay practical, respectful, and user-controlled.

## Self-avatar scene

Show a status panel with soft UI, gentle copy, and an action button.

Do not frame the panel as a clinical system.

## Relationship scene

Use memory objects, place markers, initials, photo fragments, and soft silhouettes.

Do not frame the scene as replacing a real person.

## Workforce scene

Show preparation, organization, suggestions, review, and approval.

The user should remain clearly in control.

## Passport scene

Use vaults, keys, gates, and clear controls to prove ownership and boundaries.
