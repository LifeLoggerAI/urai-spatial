# URAI First Replay Film — Cut One Production Plan

## Goal

Create a fast cinematic prototype trailer that makes URAI understandable before a traditional explainer would.

## Working title

Your Life Is a World

## Runtime

120 seconds.

## Placement

`/focus` → user taps Replay → cinematic onboarding film → create-world CTA.

## Core sequence

1. Real life pressure.
2. Open URAI.
3. Tap orb and open chat.
4. Tap self-avatar and see current state.
5. Enter ground world.
6. See private AI workforce and council roles.
7. Ascend through sky.
8. Enter Life Map.
9. Reveal relationship and legacy constellation.
10. Open Focus memory seed.
11. Tap Replay Memory.
12. Enter Replay.
13. See Mirror and Passport trust montage.
14. End on Create your world.

## Core lines

- Chat is the doorway. Your world is the interface.
- Less managing. More living.
- URAI handles the noise.
- Models with roles. Access with permission.
- Legacy stays protected. Presence requires permission.
- Replay is not just watching. It is entering.
- Your life data belongs to you.
