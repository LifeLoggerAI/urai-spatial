# Tier-0 World Navigation Canon

Status: proposed canonical foundation on `feat/tier0-world-navigation-canon-20260716`.

## Founder rule

URAI features are destinations inside a persistent world. Routes synchronize and deep-link world state; they do not replace the world with standalone webpages.

No future feature may introduce a competing primary navigation model without an explicit founder override.

## Living World

The calm above-ground Home is reserved for sky, ground, the user avatar, the orb, distant people, emotional weather, and subtle living-world signals. It must not become a feature dashboard or a plaza of permanent destination portals.

## Ground Gateway

The ground is the canonical entrance to Hidden Infrastructure. Pointer, touch, keyboard, gaze, and accessible-equivalent activation must converge on the same travel request.

The transition must:

1. preserve a Home camera checkpoint and selected context;
2. acknowledge the activation through the orb or environment;
3. lower the camera and open the surface;
4. reveal the infrastructure world without a blank route flash;
5. support deterministic ESC/back reversal; and
6. restore the prior Home state on return.

## Hidden Infrastructure

The first registry contains:

- Hidden Infrastructure hub
- Reflection Realm
- Ownership Vault
- Consent Sanctuary
- Emotional Atlas
- Focus Chamber
- Replay Theater

Future dreams, goals, AI people, simulations, and marketplace systems register as world destinations instead of standalone landing pages.

## Accessibility and safety

Environmental interaction is primary, not exclusive. Keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion, recovery, consent, export, deletion, and privacy controls retain conventional accessible equivalents where precision is required.

## Release boundary

This canon does not authorize deployment by itself. Promotion requires exact-head typecheck, build, focused contracts, navigation E2E, visual proof, accessibility proof, protected deployment, post-deploy smoke, and a verified rollback point.
