# Screenshot Capture Checklist

Every screenshot must use sample data and must avoid private account details, raw IDs, debug UI, broken images, and placeholder assets.

## Core Screenshots

- Genesis home.
- Orb Companion open.
- Passport overview.
- AI Context review.
- Life Map Galaxy.
- Life Map star detail.
- Ground Garden.
- Ground detail.
- Mirror view.
- Shadow consent gate.
- Legacy consent gate or sample Legacy.
- Export review.
- Settings.
- Onboarding welcome.
- Demo landing.
- Waitlist success.

## Capture Rules

- Use sample data.
- Capture mobile and desktop.
- Do not show browser debug console.
- Do not show private account info.
- Do not show raw IDs.
- Do not show placeholder assets.
- Do not show broken images.
- Make the sample-data disclosure visible where practical.

## Recommended Sizes

- Desktop: 1920x1080.
- Mobile: 1170x2532 or similar.
- Square crop: 1080x1080.
- Vertical story: 1080x1920.
