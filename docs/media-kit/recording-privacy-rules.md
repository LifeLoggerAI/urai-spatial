# Recording Privacy Rules

Use these rules for every URAI Genesis launch recording, screenshot, GIF, social clip, fundraiser asset, investor demo, and press visual.

## Required Rules

- Record only demo/sample mode unless the account state is deliberately sanitized.
- Do not show real private data.
- Do not show real user account identifiers.
- Do not show raw Firebase console.
- Do not show the admin panel unless recording a founder-only operations video.
- Do not show environment variables.
- Do not show API keys.
- Do not show Companion messages containing private content.
- Do not show Shadow content beyond the protected consent gate.
- Do not show Legacy real content.
- Do not show raw export files containing private content.
- Blur browser bookmarks, email, tabs, and system notifications if needed.

## Required Visible Disclosure

"This demo uses sample data."

## Claim Boundaries

- Do not make medical, therapy, diagnosis, or lie-detection claims.
- Do not describe URAI as surveillance or always-on monitoring.
- Describe URAI as permissioned, reflective, symbolic, and user-controlled.

## Preflight

Before recording, close private tabs, turn off notifications, use a demo-safe browser profile, confirm the sample-data disclosure is visible, and verify that Passport, Shadow, Legacy, and Exports are not exposing private content.
