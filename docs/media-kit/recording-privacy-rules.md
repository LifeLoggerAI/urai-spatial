# Recording Privacy Rules

Use these rules for every URAI launch recording, screenshot, GIF, social clip, fundraiser asset, investor demo, and press visual.

## Required Rules

- Record only demo/sample mode unless the account state is deliberately sanitized and approved.
- Do not show real private data or third-party information.
- Do not show real user account identifiers.
- Do not show Firebase, cloud, analytics, support, or admin consoles.
- Do not show environment variables, credentials, tokens, or API keys.
- Do not show Companion messages containing private content.
- Do not show protected Shadow or Legacy content beyond the consent gate.
- Do not show raw export files containing private content.
- Blur browser bookmarks, email, tabs, notifications, and device identifiers when needed.
- Record the exact build or deployed SHA used for the visual.

## Required Visible Disclosure

> This demo uses sample data. Some capabilities use fallback-safe behavior and remain production-certification pending.

## Claim Boundaries

- Do not make medical, treatment, therapy, diagnosis, lie-detection, mind-reading, or emotional-certainty claims.
- Do not describe URAI as surveillance or always-on monitoring.
- Do not claim persistent personal memory, active providers, production certification, or physical-device certification without exact evidence.
- Describe URAI as permission-first, reflective, spatial, symbolic, and user-controlled.
- Describe export, deletion, revocation, and personal-memory behavior as design goals unless the exact demonstrated build has authenticated end-to-end proof.

## Preflight

Before recording:

1. Close private tabs and turn off notifications.
2. Use a demo-safe browser profile and sample data.
3. Confirm the required disclosure is visible.
4. Confirm Passport, Shadow, Legacy, and export surfaces expose no private content.
5. Record the exact SHA, URL, date, device, and browser.
6. Verify the spoken script against the current claims matrix and deployment receipts.
