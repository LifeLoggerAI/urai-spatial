## Status vocabulary

- **VERIFIED LIVE** — exact deployed SHA, target, public behavior, and rollback evidence exist.
- **VERIFIED IN REPOSITORY** — source, tests, or documentation exist and are tied to a commit, but live deployment is not proved.
- **IMPLEMENTED BUT NOT DEPLOYED** — reviewed implementation exists on a branch or pull request and awaits merge/deploy evidence.
- **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED** — meaningful source exists but acceptance criteria are incomplete.
- **BLOCKED** — completion requires external authority, credentials, billing, legal approval, device access, or a decision that cannot be truthfully inferred.
- **ROADMAP** — defined future capability without complete implementation evidence.
- **REJECTED OR OBSOLETE** — superseded or explicitly non-canonical surface.

## Receipt rule

No item may move to **VERIFIED LIVE** without a receipt containing the exact commit, workflow, tests, deployment target, deployed SHA, public verification, rollback SHA, and remaining caveats.
