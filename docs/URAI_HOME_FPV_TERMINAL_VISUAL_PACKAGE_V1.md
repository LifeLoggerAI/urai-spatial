## 6. Material Bible

### Soil / organic ground
- roughness target: high, varied;
- metalness: zero;
- normal/detail: close-range legible without noisy procedural repetition;
- moisture: restrained localized variation only;
- avoid: flat color, tiling, uniform gloss.

### Rock / weathered stone
- roughness: medium-high;
- metalness: zero to trace mineral response only;
- edge wear: physical, non-gamey;
- avoid: uniform bevels, repeated low-poly facets in hero view.

### Path
- readable against adjacent terrain without glowing outline;
- slightly compacted / worn material response;
- no UI-like emissive path strip.

### Architecture / personalized objects
- physically plausible wood/stone/glass/metal response;
- no generic glowing sci-fi blocks standing in for meaning;
- provenance determines autobiographical claim.

### Vegetation
- matte to soft-specular leaf response;
- asynchronous wind variation;
- reject repeated sphere-canopy silhouettes in final hero art.

### Orb
- material silhouette must survive bloom disabled;
- field/membrane transmission is restrained;
- internal authored model, non-spherical heart, stabilizer geometry and fragments remain legible;
- no generic glass-ball identity.

### Passport
Current candidate becomes `protected-custody-stone-v2`: tactile layered mineral shells, restrained custody seam and embedded memory inclusion. No tablet, screen slab, kiosk, settings gear, passport book, pedestal or portal language.

### Global Emotional Field Earth
- physically plausible Earth identity first;
- aggregate field is an overlay, not replacement planet identity;
- unavailable/suppressed states must remain visually truthful;
- no individual dots or exact locations.

### Contextual UI
- world-preserving, dismissible, high-contrast;
- no permanent HUD;
- no full-screen dashboard as default Home state.

## 7. Lighting Bible

### A — canonical daylight
Natural atmospheric key dominates; Orb local light is secondary. Preserve contact shadows and material roughness.

### B — evening
Warm/cool balance follows actual sky state, not universal orange grade. Preserve path and face/object readability.

### C — night
Readable silhouettes and local pools of light; no default blue-purple cyberpunk wash. Orb contributes locally but never becomes the scene sun.

### D — Personal Emotional Weather
Atmosphere/light modulation may shift density, softness, directional clarity and restrained color temperature without replacing geography or implying diagnosis.

### E — reduced stimulation
Reduce particle density, contrast fluctuation, macro light transients and Orb motion intensity while preserving semantic hierarchy.

## 17. Accessibility reference states
