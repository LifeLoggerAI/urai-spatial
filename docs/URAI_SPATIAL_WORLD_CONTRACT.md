# URAI Spatial World Contract

URAI Spatial is not a dashboard, game, or traditional app. It is a place: a persistent reflective world where a person's life is represented through space, light, motion, and relationships instead of menus and forms.

This contract turns the URAI Spatial philosophy into implementation rules that can be checked in code, product review, design review, QA, and asset generation.

## Core promise

A user should feel three things within seconds of arrival:

1. **Oriented** — I know where I am.
2. **Calm** — nothing is attacking my attention.
3. **Curious** — I want to move deeper.

The environment must communicate this before the user reads a full paragraph.

## Five pillars

| Pillar | Meaning | Implementation rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sky | Memory and possibility | Memories appear as stars in layered atmospheric depth, never as dashboard cards. |
| Ground | Reality and lived life | Present life appears as reachable terrain, paths, objects, places, and routines. |
| Orb | Conscious identity | The Orb is an ambient awareness anchor, breathing softly and influencing nearby light. |
| Avatar | Embodied presence | The avatar communicates presence through subtle posture, breathing, gaze, and weight shifts. |
| Camera | Awareness | Camera movement is intentional, calm, predictable, and never action-game-like. |

Nothing exists purely for decoration. Every visual element must reinforce at least one pillar.

## Route meaning

| Route | Spatial meaning | Required feeling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` and `/home` | Threshold between ground, sky, Orb, avatar, and routes | Stable arrival, world-scale orientation |
| `/life-map` | Looking up into the memory sky | Vastness, perspective, constellation discovery |
| `/ground` | Walking through present life | Stability, reachability, active lived reality |
| `/focus` | Complete attention inside one selected star | Calm focus without isolation |
| `/replay` | Entering a remembered moment | Cinematic passage, not a video panel |
| `/mirror` | Pattern reflection | Private interpretation, not public scoring |
| `/passport` | Ownership and consent | Secure identity, non-performative control |
| `/status` | System state as world layer | Operational clarity without dashboard takeover |

## Sky contract

The sky is the user's autobiographical field.

Required qualities:

- No visible edge or wallpaper-dome feeling.
- Layered depth: near stars feel reachable, far stars feel soft and atmospheric.
- Stars are placed by relationships: time periods, people, places, recurring themes, emotional clusters, and shared experiences.
- Constellations emerge from data relationships; they are not random decoration.
- Weather-like emotional modulation changes slowly over time.
- The sky never flashes for attention.

Emotional modulation must be subtle:

- Calm: clearer visibility, soft blue atmospheric depth.
- Uncertainty: light mist, muted contrast.
- Grief: softened distant visibility and quieter particles.
- Growth: increased depth and brightness over time.
- Recovery: gradual warmth and clarity, never instant gamified reward.

## Ground contract

The ground is present life made physical.

Required qualities:

- Ground is visible or implied from Home.
- Ground feels reachable, not like a separate website section.
- Present-life entities become spatial objects, paths, structures, rooms, and terrain features.
- Relationships, work, family, health, places, projects, responsibilities, and routines should live as explorable world objects.
- No floating admin-card takeover.

The ground answers: **Where am I today?**

## Orb contract

The Orb represents conscious identity.

Required qualities:

- The Orb should breathe through light, not theatrical animation.
- It must remain an ambient anchor, not a mascot or logo.
- It can pulse for insight, soften during fatigue, and stabilize during calm.
- Its light subtly influences nearby space.
- It should be minimal because identity should not be noisy.

The Orb communicates: **You are here.**

## Avatar contract

The avatar reminds the user that they inhabit the life-world.

Required qualities:

- Natural breathing.
- Gentle idle weight shifts.
- Subtle head and gaze motion.
- Relaxed posture by default.
- Emotional posture is understated: confidence opens shoulders, stress adds slight tension, reflection slows motion, relief relaxes the body.

No exaggerated theatrical emotion loops.

## Camera contract

The camera is awareness.

Required qualities:

- No jerks, snaps, shock zooms, rapid spins, or action-game camera language.
- Movement uses smooth acceleration and deceleration.
- Idle movement is almost imperceptible breathing.
- Transitions are gentle dolly moves or trackable spatial passage.
- Selecting a star is approach, not click-to-panel.
- Returning Home widens context instead of hard resetting.
- Reduced-motion users must bypass or simplify camera travel.

## Memory selection contract

Selecting a memory star means the user is approaching a remembered place.

Required sequence:

1. Selected star brightens slightly.
2. Nearby stars dim softly but remain present.
3. Camera eases toward the chosen star.
4. Depth increases and background stars drift outward.
5. Audio narrows and quiets.
6. Focus or Replay opens through spatial passage, not a hard panel.

## Focus contract

Focus is complete attention without isolation.

Required qualities:

- Peripheral stars fade but remain available as context.
- Particles slow.
- Ambient audio quiets.
- Depth of field narrows gently.
- The chosen memory becomes dominant by contrast, not by removing the world.

## Replay contract

Replay is entering a remembered moment.

Required qualities:

- The star expands into an environment.
- The camera passes through the star surface.
- Lighting transitions continuously into the memory space.
- Replay returns by widening back out into the prior sky context.
- Replay must never feel like opening a video player inside a dashboard.

## Lighting, color, and audio

Lighting carries meaning while preserving comfort and legibility.

Rules:

- Primary light comes from the environment.
- Secondary light comes from the Orb.
- Bloom highlights important objects only.
- Darkness means quiet, not danger.
- Color is suggestive, not deterministic or judgmental.
- Spatial audio should behave like air in the place, not a manipulative soundtrack.

## Accessibility and safety

URAI Spatial must support reflection without overwhelming users.

Required safeguards:

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Avoid rapid peripheral motion.
- Avoid forced long camera moves.
- Preserve keyboard and screen-reader route access.
- Provide textual route equivalents without making text the primary interface.
- Never label emotional state as fixed truth; present it as reflective atmosphere.

## Non-negotiables

URAI Spatial must not regress into:

- Dashboard cards as the primary world model.
- Random decorative stars.
- Action-game camera movement.
- Loud gamified emotional scoring.
- Isolated route pages with no sense of one continuous place.
- Asset showcases that do not serve the five pillars.

The final product should feel like architecture for reflection: technology fading into the background while the user inhabits a calm, cinematic life-world.
