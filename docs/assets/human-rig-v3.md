# URAI Human Rig V3

Seven actual skinned GLBs with JOINTS_0/WEIGHTS_0, inverse bind matrices, a 17-joint humanoid skeleton, five named animation clips, embedded PBR albedo textures, continuous body topology, and relaxed bent-arm rest silhouettes. 5:4 framing; meters; Y-up.

Runtime integration: six distinct Council humans are rendered and selectable in `/council`; the seventh Home human is consumed by the canonical Home `PresenceRig`. All seven remain `pending-final-review` candidates until compression/LOD, rendered motion proof, and final visual approval are complete.
