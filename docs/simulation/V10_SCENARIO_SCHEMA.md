# V10 Scenario Schema

V10 scenarios are advisory simulations only. They do not predict individual behavior or control users.

## Fields

- id
- title
- timeHorizon
- actors
- institutions
- technologies
- constraints
- risks
- benefits
- unknowns
- safetyBoundary

## Required safety boundary

Every scenario must include:

- no individual prediction
- no manipulation engine
- no hidden behavioral control
- no privacy-invasive modeling
- advisory simulation only
