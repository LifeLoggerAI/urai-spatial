# V10 Safety Boundary

V10 is a strategic simulation and research layer.

It must never become:

- an individual prediction engine
- a manipulation engine
- hidden behavioral control
- privacy-invasive surveillance
- an automated governance authority

V10 is allowed to:

- model high-level scenarios
- compare long-horizon risks and benefits
- generate advisory reports
- identify uncertainty
- document safety boundaries

V10 output must remain advisory, auditable, and privacy-preserving.
