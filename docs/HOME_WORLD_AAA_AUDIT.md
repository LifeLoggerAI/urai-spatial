# Home World AAA Audit

Current screenshots show `/` and `/home` still reading as a left-heavy landing page over a dim scenic backdrop. The Home World needs one scene owner, finished depth, working route portals, a single orb companion, and no duplicate fake worlds.

## Brutal current-state findings

- The first read is typography and buttons, not a spatial sanctuary.
- The ground is present but reads as a dark shelf/pill rather than terrain with weight, perspective, route stones, and reflection.
- The sky has atmosphere but not enough authored memory ceiling: too few visible stars, weak parallax, weak horizon story.
- The central orb/avatar stack is visually close to the canon, but it still reads like a decorative object because the orb is not clearly an interactive companion.
- The route controls are mostly conventional CTA chips; they need to feel connected to ground portals.
- The top-left Life Map status circle is confusing because it appears partially off-canvas and competes with the hero.
- Old imported visual layers still risk stacked-world composition and duplicate ground/orb treatments.
- Mobile currently sacrifices too much of the authored world rather than simplifying it.

## Production bar

Home must feel like Genesis: sky, horizon, ground, avatar presence, orb companion, and route portals all belonging to one cinematic spatial world. It must remain static-export safe, keyboard accessible, reduced-motion aware, and route-complete.
