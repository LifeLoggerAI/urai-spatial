## Non-negotiable product outcome

Home must stop reading as an authored wallpaper with translucent geometry and controls layered over it. The final result must present one coherent environment with consistent perspective, scale, lighting, fog, materials, occlusion, shadows, spatial depth, and interaction.

The final Home must remove or replace:

- the darkened full-screen authored image as the dominant visual owner;
- generic sphere-and-icon memory bubbles;
- debug-like rings, cones, cylinders, capsule figures, and proof geometry;
- the giant movement prompt and persistent direct-destination pill row as dominant visual elements;
- the placeholder capsule-and-sphere embodied self;
- ungrounded Orb and threshold visuals that lack contact light, shadow, reflection, or environmental response.

The final Home must preserve:

- calm continuous movement;
- keyboard, click-to-walk, touch, reduced-motion, and no-WebGL access;
- semantic direct access to Orb, Ground, and Life Map;
- the canonical experience chain `Home → Ascent → Life Map → Focus → Replay` and reverse unwind;
- privacy-safe disclosed sample/demo boundaries;
- exact-head evidence and protected release controls.

## Acceptance boundary

This branch cannot be merged because tests pass alone. Acceptance requires fresh exact-head desktop and mobile screenshots and movement recordings, all required workflows green, protected deployment evidence, public runtime verification, and explicit founder approval of the deployed visual result as the final no-placeholder Home.
