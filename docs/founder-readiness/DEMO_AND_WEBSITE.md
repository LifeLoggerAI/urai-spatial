# Demo Runbook and Website Copy

## Demo principles

- Show one coherent journey rather than every route.
- Use sample data and say so.
- Do not open admin, Firebase, console, environment, or private account screens.
- Do not claim production certification, persistent personal memory, active providers, or device certification unless current receipts prove it.
- End with one concrete next step.

## 60-second event demo

### Setup

Open the stable public demo or current verified route before the conversation. Turn off notifications. Confirm the browser is not showing private session state.

### Script

**0-10 seconds — Home**

> This is URAI. Instead of starting with a dashboard or chat box, you enter a private world organized around your life.

**10-25 seconds — Life Map**

> Here, memories and life chapters are represented spatially. The point is not decoration. It gives a person a way to see relationships, time, goals, and important experiences as one connected system.

**25-42 seconds — Focus**

> When you choose a memory or chapter, the system moves into Focus. This narrows the world around what matters right now without losing where it came from.

**42-52 seconds — Replay**

> From there, Replay turns the selected material into a source-aware life story or reflective experience. In the final system, the person controls the underlying evidence and permissions.

**52-60 seconds — Close**

> This demo uses sample data. The current web experience is substantial, and we are closing the production, privacy, and deployment evidence required for a public beta.

Then ask:

> Which part is most relevant to you: memory, identity, reflection, focus, or the spatial interface itself?

## Three-minute investor demo

### 1. The problem — 20 seconds

> Human life is fragmented across photos, messages, files, calendars, social platforms, and private memory. Existing software stores pieces or answers questions, but it does not give a person a coherent interface for their own life.

### 2. Home and Ground — 30 seconds

> URAI begins with a grounded personal world, not a feed. Ground is the operating environment: where rituals, priorities, and human-approved actions can eventually connect without turning the person into a productivity score.

### 3. Life Map — 45 seconds

> The Life Map is the core spatial interface. Memories become artifacts, relationships can form constellations, goals become destinations, and unresolved chapters can remain protected. The map is designed to preserve context and continuity instead of flattening life into folders.

### 4. Focus and Replay — 45 seconds

> Selecting an item moves into Focus and then Replay. The current product demonstrates the route and visual journey. The production goal is evidence-backed reconstruction where the user can see what came from a source, what they added, and what the AI inferred.

### 5. Mirror and Passport — 35 seconds

> Mirror is reflective, not diagnostic. Passport is the permission and identity layer: what can open, what can be remembered, what can leave, and what remains closed. That control model is central to the product, not an afterthought.

### 6. Current truth — 25 seconds

> We have a substantial V1 fallback/demo spatial web experience, canonical routes, runtime architecture, privacy gates, asset governance, adaptive rendering, and release infrastructure. We are finishing exact deployment certification, final production assets, authenticated data isolation, and end-to-end user-data proof.

### 7. Ask — 20 seconds

> We are looking for capital and design partners to move from working experience to a secure public beta, validate retention in a narrow initial use case, and build the evidence required for broader personal intelligence.

## Demo failure fallback

If the live experience fails, do not improvise technical claims.

Say:

> The live build is not loading reliably on this connection. I have a recorded walkthrough and a one-page product map. The important thing is the journey and the permission model; I will send you the live link after the event.

Maintain an offline video and screenshot sequence covering Home, Life Map, Focus, Replay, Mirror, Passport, and Status.

## Homepage copy

### Hero

**Your life should not look like a dashboard.**

URAI is building a private spatial operating system for memory, identity, reflection, focus, and personal direction.

Primary CTA: **Enter the demo**

Secondary CTA: **Join the beta**

Demo disclosure: **The current public experience uses sample data and remains certification-pending.**

### Problem section

Your memories live in photos. Your relationships live in messages. Your plans live in calendars and notes. Your identity is scattered across systems that were never designed to help you see the whole picture.

URAI brings those parts into one human-centered world without reducing a life to a feed, score, or folder tree.

### Product section

**Life Map** — Explore memories, relationships, goals, and chapters as a connected spatial system.

**Focus** — Move from the whole map into the one thing that matters now.

**Replay** — Reconstruct meaningful stories with clear boundaries between source material, user input, and AI interpretation.

**Mirror** — Reflect without medical, diagnostic, or surveillance framing.

**Passport** — Control what opens, remembers, reflects, exports, or stays closed.

**Status** — See what is implemented, what is verified, and what is still pending.

### Trust section

URAI is being designed around permission, provenance, export, deletion, revocation, and evidence-backed claims. A route existing in source is not treated as proof that a provider, device, or backend capability is live.

### Current-status section

The public product currently demonstrates a substantial V1 spatial web experience with fallback-safe behavior. Production certification, final provider assets, authenticated persistence, and physical-device proof remain gated until receipts verify them.

### Final CTA

**Help shape a new interface for human memory and identity.**

Join the early beta, explore a design-partner pilot, or request an investor demonstration.

## One-page product description

URAI is a privacy-first spatial operating system for a person's life. It is designed to transform scattered memories, relationships, goals, experiences, and personal evidence into a navigable world controlled by the user.

The experience moves from Home and Ground into a Life Map, then into Focus, Replay, Mirror, Passport, and Status. Each surface has a distinct role: orientation, intentional action, spatial memory, narrative reconstruction, reflection, permissions, and evidence.

The current repository contains a substantial fallback/demo spatial web experience and the infrastructure needed to verify a production release. The immediate objective is a narrow, secure public beta with exact deployment proof, route parity, final visual assets, authenticated user isolation, and measurable user value.

URAI is not positioned as therapy, diagnosis, lie detection, surveillance, or an autonomous substitute for human judgment.
