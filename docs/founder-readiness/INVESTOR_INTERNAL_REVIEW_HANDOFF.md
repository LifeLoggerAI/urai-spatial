# Investor Internal Review Handoff

Date: 2026-07-10
Status: internal working material; not an offering document

## Selected initial wedge hypothesis

**Guided reflection and decision continuity for founders and independent creators.**

This is a hypothesis for controlled testing, not a validated market, traction, retention, willingness-to-pay, or revenue claim.

### Why this is the first hypothesis

- The current V1 journey already supports orientation, a spatial Life Map, selection, Focus, Replay, Mirror, Passport, and Status.
- The founder-event package can demonstrate the journey with sample data without requiring real personal-memory persistence.
- Founders and independent creators regularly work across decisions, projects, relationships, stories, and long-running context, which makes continuity a concrete problem to investigate.
- The cohort can be recruited narrowly and evaluated through comprehension, return intent, repeated use, trust, and journey completion.
- This wedge does not require medical, caregiving, veteran, child, biometric, wearable, or physical-XR claims for the first beta.

### What must be proven

1. Target users understand the spatial journey without extensive coaching.
2. They can identify one recurring reflection or decision-continuity job.
3. They return to the same thread more than once.
4. They understand sample data, permission boundaries, and what is not persistent.
5. The spatial model creates value beyond a conventional document, notes app, or chat transcript.
6. At least one future paid or sponsored workflow can be stated without exploiting private context.

### What is not claimed

- No validated market size.
- No product-market fit.
- No retention or conversion result.
- No authenticated personal-memory persistence.
- No proprietary-model ownership.
- No production certification.
- No final entity, capitalization, IP-chain, valuation, or financing conclusion.

## De-risking milestone request framework

Any resource or funding request should map to a measurable result:

1. **Release truth:** exact SHA verified, deployed through the protected workflow, publicly identifiable, and rollback-ready.
2. **Trust foundation:** authenticated ownership, tenant isolation, deletion, export, revocation, and audit tests for enabled real-user data.
3. **Controlled cohort:** 15–30 approved testers linked to one exact build, with no open P0 issue.
4. **Wedge evidence:** repeated founder/creator use, comprehension, trust, return intent, and a clear recurring job.
5. **Final V1 presentation:** approved selected assets, provenance, accessibility, mobile performance, and route screenshots.
6. **External review:** appropriate security, privacy, accessibility, legal, and claims review.

## Public or repository-safe materials

- Claim-safe one-pager and investor package.
- Exact public Status and evidence links after deployment.
- Sample-data demo and screenshots.
- Founder biography that uses approved identity language.
- Public repository map and technical architecture at an appropriate level.
- Redacted milestone and risk summaries.

## Confidential materials requiring controlled storage

Do not place the following in the public repository:

- Cap table, SAFEs, notes, financing history, valuation discussions, bank information, tax records, or detailed financial forecasts.
- Entity formation, good-standing, ownership, board, officer, member, or authorization records.
- IP assignments, invention records, license agreements, patent strategy, trademark advice, or chain-of-title evidence.
- Signatures, government IDs, addresses, personal phone numbers, birth dates, or banking/payment data.
- Raw customer, partner, donor, investor, participant, tester, or employee information.
- Unredacted contracts, legal advice, privileged correspondence, diligence responses, security findings, credentials, environment files, or service-account material.
- Real private user memories, communications, recordings, biometrics, health information, location history, or third-party information.

## Meeting boundary

Until receipt-backed deployment and controlled-beta evidence exist, use this framing:

> URAI has a substantial sample-data spatial web experience and a testable founder/creator decision-continuity hypothesis. The immediate work is to establish exact production proof, complete real-user trust controls, and test whether a narrow cohort returns to the journey for a recurring job.

Do not publish financing terms, investment-return expectations, ownership assertions, market-size figures, traction metrics, customer names, pilot claims, or completion/certification statements without current supporting evidence and appropriate approval.

## Exact human handoff

1. Product owner: approve or replace the founder/creator wedge hypothesis before cohort recruitment.
2. Release owner: provide the exact tested/deployed SHA and immutable receipt before the demo is used in investor outreach.
3. Legal/corporate owner: review entity, authority, IP, confidentiality, and solicitation materials in controlled storage.
4. Finance owner: provide a private assumption-backed model and milestone-linked use of funds.
5. Claims approver: sign the current public claims matrix and define the next-review trigger.
