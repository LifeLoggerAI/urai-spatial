## Selected wedge hypothesis

Primary initial test hypothesis: **founder decision continuity**.

This is not a validated market claim. It was selected for the next research cycle because it:

- can be demonstrated with synthetic or low-risk test data;
- aligns with founder-event and design-partner distribution already being prepared;
- tests whether Life Map, Focus, and Replay improve continuity across decisions and chapters;
- avoids medical, diagnostic, caregiver, biometric, location, and physical-device claims;
- provides a concrete recurring-use question without narrowing the long-term platform vision prematurely.

Evidence required before describing the wedge as validated:

1. A defined founder cohort tied to one exact build.
2. Repeated use to recover context or reflect on decisions.
3. Correct understanding of sample data, permissions, and AI/source boundaries.
4. Return intent and willingness to continue or pay.
5. Evidence that the spatial model adds value beyond a conventional journal, document, or chat interface.

Alternative wedges remain hypotheses and must not be presented as active markets without equivalent evidence.

## Public versus confidential separation

Safe for the public repository after claims review:

- sample-data screenshots and demo video;
- claim-safe one-pager;
- public Status and evidence boundaries;
- high-level milestones and risk categories;
- founder biography only after authorized identity wording is approved.

Keep in an approved access-controlled private location:

- cap table and financing history;
- bank, tax, payroll, identity, address, signature, and government records;
- confidential valuation, terms, investor pipeline, or negotiation notes;
- entity formation and good-standing records not intended for publication;
- IP assignments, contributor agreements, unpublished filings, legal opinions, and chain-of-title details;
- material contracts, confidential pilots, private user research, and security findings;
- credentials, environment files, raw logs, or user data.

Do not create a `private-data-room` directory in this public repository.
