# Claims and Legal Guardrails

This document is an operating checklist, not legal advice. Counsel should approve entity, ownership, securities, privacy, healthcare, research, employment, and licensing language before formal use.

## Green claims

These may be used when they remain consistent with current `STATUS.md`, `EVIDENCE.md`, and deployment receipts.

- URAI is building a spatial operating system for memory, identity, reflection, focus, and personal direction.
- The canonical public product path includes Home, Ground, Life Map, Focus, Replay, Mirror, Passport, and Status.
- The repository contains a substantial V1 fallback/demo spatial web experience.
- The current experience can be demonstrated with sample data.
- The product is designed around permission-first and user-controlled interaction.
- The repository includes privacy gates, fallback behavior, release automation, asset governance, and evidence infrastructure.
- URAI distinguishes implemented source surfaces from provider-active, device-certified, and production-certified capabilities.
- Mirror is a reflective experience and is not positioned as diagnosis or therapy.

## Yellow claims

Use only with an immediate qualifier.

### "URAI is live"

Use:

> A substantial public demo is reachable; production certification and exact deployment proof remain pending.

### "URAI remembers your life"

Use:

> URAI is designed to support user-controlled memory experiences. Persistent, authenticated personal-memory behavior must not be represented as production-proven until backend, privacy, deletion, export, and live-smoke receipts exist.

### "URAI reconstructs memories"

Use:

> Replay is designed for source-aware narrative reconstruction and currently demonstrates the experience with sample or fallback data.

### "URAI works in VR or AR"

Use:

> URAI includes WebXR and Quest preparation in source, but physical-device certification remains pending.

### "URAI uses AI to predict your future"

Use:

> URAI explores user-controlled possibilities and planning scenarios. It does not predict fate or guarantee outcomes.

### "URAI owns extensive IP"

Use only after counsel confirms the current entity, chain of title, filing status, assignments, and permitted wording. Separate filed, pending, registered, claimed, drafted, and conceptual material.

### "URAI is raising"

Use only with approved securities language and a clear audience. Do not publish terms, returns, valuation promises, or investment solicitation language without counsel review.

## Red claims

Do not use without new evidence and appropriate legal, clinical, privacy, or regulatory review.

- Fully production-certified V1 through V100.
- Active provider-backed V2/V3 assets when receipts show they are not promoted.
- Device-certified Quest or WebXR support without physical-device evidence.
- Medical diagnosis, treatment, therapy replacement, crisis intervention, or clinical outcome claims.
- Lie detection, mind reading, emotional certainty, or psychological truth detection.
- Always-on surveillance or passive monitoring without explicit, narrow consent.
- Autonomous real-world actions that the current system does not execute and verify.
- Persistent private memory, wearable data, body-signal integration, or Firestore isolation without live proof.
- Guaranteed investment return, guaranteed market size, guaranteed patent coverage, or guaranteed regulatory outcome.
- Ownership claims that ignore unresolved historical entity names or assignments.

## Required disclosure language

### Public demo

> This demo uses sample data. Some capabilities are represented through fallback-safe behavior and remain production-certification pending.

### Investor demo

> The demonstration shows the current spatial product experience and source-implemented journey. Provider activation, authenticated persistence, physical-device certification, and production deployment claims are separately receipt-gated.

### Research or health-adjacent discussion

> URAI is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, therapy replacement, or emergency service in its current form. Any future research or health use would require appropriate governance, consent, validation, and review.

## Entity and ownership cleanup checklist

Counsel or an authorized corporate officer should produce one dated entity registry covering:

- Current legal name of each active URAI entity.
- Jurisdiction, formation date, registration number, and status.
- Founders, managers, members, directors, or officers as applicable.
- Assumed names and historical names.
- Ownership of domains, repositories, source code, design assets, datasets, contracts, trademarks, patent filings, copyrights, and trade secrets.
- Assignment history from individuals or earlier entities.
- Contractor and contributor invention-assignment coverage.
- Open-source and commercial-asset license inventory.
- Foundation versus for-profit boundaries.
- Which entity may sign pilots, accept grants, employ staff, license IP, and raise capital.
- Which historical documents must remain unchanged and which current documents should be updated.

## Legal-document handling rules

- Never rewrite an executed historical agreement merely to match the current entity name.
- Preserve original dates, parties, and signatures.
- Use a superseding agreement, assignment, novation, amendment, or correction only when authorized.
- Do not expose private addresses, personal email addresses, signatures, IDs, cap tables, or confidential deal terms in the public repository.
- Keep public marketing copy separate from confidential investor and counsel materials.
- Mark drafts clearly.
- Record the source and approval date for every material legal claim.

## Privacy readiness questions

Before real-user memory is enabled, verify:

- Which data is collected and why?
- What is the lawful and user-understood basis for each use?
- Can a user view, correct, export, delete, and revoke access?
- Is every record scoped to an authenticated user and tenant?
- Are event envelopes ownership-bound and mismatch-rejecting?
- Are logs, analytics, screenshots, support tools, and backups included in deletion and retention policy?
- Are sensitive layers closed by default?
- Can AI access only the context the user has opened?
- Are inferred statements visibly distinguished from sourced facts and user-authored content?
- Is there a safe failure mode when a provider or model is unavailable?
