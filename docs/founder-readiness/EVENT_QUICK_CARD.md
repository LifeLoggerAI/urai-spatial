# URAI Founder Event Quick Card

Use this as the phone-screen version before and during a founder, funding, or demo event.

## One sentence

> URAI turns memories, relationships, goals, and life chapters into a private spatial world you can navigate instead of another feed or dashboard.

## Opening

> Hey, I’m Adam. I’m building URAI. We’re creating a new way for people to interact with their own memories, relationships, goals, and life story. What are you working on?

## Thirty seconds

> Most technology stores pieces of your life but never helps you see the whole picture. URAI is building a spatial operating system where memories can become places, relationships can form constellations, goals can become destinations, and the user controls what the system can access, remember, reflect, or export. We have a substantial web experience now and are closing the evidence required for a secure public beta.

## Ask to show it

> I have a one-minute demo using sample data. Want to see it?

## One-minute demo words

**Home**

> Instead of a dashboard or chat box, you enter a private world organized around your life.

**Life Map**

> Memories and chapters are represented spatially so a person can see relationships, time, goals, and experiences as one connected system.

**Focus**

> Choosing one item narrows the world around what matters without losing where it came from.

**Replay**

> Replay turns selected material into a source-aware reflective story. The person controls the evidence and permissions.

**Close**

> This uses sample data. The current web experience is substantial, and we are finishing production, privacy, and deployment proof for public beta.

## When they say, “That sounds crazy”

> It is a big vision, but the first step is concrete. Pick a memory in the Life Map, move into Focus, and watch it become a Replay. Once people experience that transition, the larger idea makes sense.

## When they ask, “Is it live?”

> A substantial public demo is reachable. I describe it as a working fallback/demo experience because final production certification, selected assets, and deeper authenticated data integration are still being completed.

## When they ask, “Are you raising?”

> We’re building investor relationships while closing the public-beta evidence gates. The financing conversation is about moving from a substantial working experience into a secure beta with final assets, real users, and measurable retention.

## Choose one ask

- “Would you be open to a 20-minute follow-up demo?”
- “Who is one person here you think should see this?”
- “Would you consider testing the beta?”
- “What milestone would you need to see before considering an investment?”
- “Could your organization be a design partner for one controlled use case?”

## Do not say

- Fully production-certified.
- V1 through V100 complete.
- Active real-user persistent memory unless current receipts prove it.
- Device-certified VR or AR.
- Medical, diagnostic, therapy, lie-detection, or surveillance claims.
- Guaranteed patents, valuation, returns, or market outcomes.

## Before walking in

- Charge phone and laptop.
- Open the exact demo route.
- Turn off notifications.
- Confirm sample data only.
- Keep an offline video ready.
- Carry one QR destination.
- Aim for three strong follow-ups.

## Follow-up message

> Great meeting you. I appreciated your perspective on [topic]. URAI is the spatial life-operating-system project I showed you: a private way to navigate memory, relationships, goals, reflection, and personal evidence. The useful next step would be a 20-minute conversation about [pilot / investment milestone / introduction].
