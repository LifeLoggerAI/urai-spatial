# URAI Beta Tester Packet

## Welcome

You are testing an early URAI spatial web experience. The goal is to learn whether the product is understandable, useful, trustworthy, and technically reliable.

This is not a medical service, therapy replacement, diagnostic tool, emergency service, lie detector, or surveillance system.

## Data rule

Use sample or low-sensitivity test information only unless a separate approved test explicitly authorizes real data.

Do not enter:

- medical or mental-health records;
- legal or financial information;
- passwords, account credentials, or government IDs;
- biometric or precise location information;
- private third-party information;
- anything you would not want included in a test report.

## What you are testing

The current test focuses on the product journey:

Home -> Life Map -> Focus -> Replay -> Passport or Privacy Controls

Some visuals and behaviors may use fallback assets or sample data. A working route does not mean every planned provider, device, or backend capability is active.

## Test instructions

Complete these tasks without coaching whenever possible:

1. Enter the experience and describe what you think Home represents.
2. Find Life Map.
3. Select one memory, chapter, or star.
4. Move into Focus.
5. Enter Replay.
6. Return from Replay without losing the selected item.
7. Find Passport or Privacy Controls.
8. Explain URAI in one sentence.
9. Identify what appeared real, simulated, inferred, or still under construction.
10. Report any moment that felt confusing, invasive, overly certain, or emotionally inappropriate.

## Feedback questions

### Understanding

- What do you think URAI is?
- Which moment made the idea clear?
- What remained confusing?
- Did any wording sound bigger than what you saw?

### Value

- What problem could this solve for you?
- Which part would make you return?
- Which part felt unnecessary?
- What one use case would you test again?

### Trust and control

- Did you understand when sample data was used?
- Did you understand what the system could access?
- Did Passport or Privacy Controls feel clear?
- Did anything feel diagnostic, manipulative, invasive, or overly certain?
- What control was missing?

### Technical experience

- Device and browser:
- Connection quality:
- Did the first screen load reliably?
- Did you see a blank screen, broken asset, or stuck transition?
- Did motion or visual density cause discomfort?
- Could you navigate by keyboard or assistive technology where needed?

### Commitment

- Would you test again?
- Would you invite one trusted person?
- Would you consider a paid or sponsored pilot for a clearly defined use case?
- What would need to change first?

## Severity guide

**P0 — Stop testing**

Private or cross-user information appears; permissions are bypassed; the wrong production build is served; a core route is unavailable; or there is an immediate safety or legal risk.

**P1 — Major**

The main journey cannot be completed, selected memory identity is lost, mobile use is blocked, Status or Privacy Controls show the wrong content, or a required data control fails.

**P2 — Moderate**

Confusing copy, intermittent transition failure, visual mismatch, performance regression, or accessibility problem with a workaround.

**P3 — Minor**

Cosmetic issue, optional polish, or future enhancement.
