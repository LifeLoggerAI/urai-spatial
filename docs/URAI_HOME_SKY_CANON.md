5. **Memory zenith** — darkest atmospheric region, never pure display black, faint relational memory lights without graphic constellation lines.

## Cloud authority

Home uses three perceptual regimes from one coherent procedural language:

1. lower distant vapor for horizon depth;
2. broken mid/high stratiform sheet for primary cloud mass;
3. sparse high filament for near-subliminal memory-pathway resemblance.

Clouds advect through world space and use different vectors. They do not rotate around the camera and do not boil. Internal density evolution is slower than bulk travel. No obvious short loop is allowed.

## Celestial authority

The Home field keeps approximately 260 total lights, divided into four perceptual classes:

- 169 physical stellar noise;

- 8 rare deep anchors.

Visible stars use circular antialiased profiles, temporal stability, size/depth variation and atmospheric extinction. Dense clouds visually occlude them. Only a sparse subset scintillates, asynchronously and at low amplitude. Reduced motion disables scintillation.

## Orb ↔ atmosphere

Orb and sky are not synchronized animation partners. Orb influence remains local: nearby moisture, particulate visibility and immediate atmospheric response may change subtly with Orb state. The entire sky must not pulse with the Orb. No energy beam, giant halo or constellation-wide response is allowed.

## Ground / horizon / sky seam

The horizon is a volume, never a hard line. Physical terrain must share aerial perspective with the atmosphere: declining contrast, desaturation, atmospheric color contamination, softened detail and distance haze. Pure-black ridge cutouts against a bright sky fail acceptance.

## Motion hierarchy

- micro: subpixel particulate / restrained scintillation;
- ambient: slow cloud translation and local haze response;
- environmental: meaningful cloud/horizon evolution over tens of seconds;
- long cycle: restrained weather convergence over minutes;
- triggered: Life Map ascent, return, or meaningful Orb/world state.

No universal sky pulse exists. Slow environmental cycles remain intentionally asynchronous.

## Ascent law

Home → Life Map is physical departure and atmospheric extinction reduction, not teleportation.

Normal sequence:

1. **Recognition** — intent acknowledged without portal geometry.
2. **Release** — camera leaves Ground softly; near haze creates parallax.
3. **Atmosphere** — cloud thickness and large-scale depth become obvious.
4. **Threshold** — teal air falls away; near-black celestial depth appears behind it.
5. **Cosmic resolve** — persistent Home precursors are recognized inside the much larger Life Map field.

No blank route frame, arbitrary fade-to-black, warp zoom or hard scene pop is acceptable.

## Reduced motion and accessibility

Reduced motion preserves visual identity while removing vestibular intensity:

- no celestial rotation;
- cloud advection reduced roughly 80–95%;
- scintillation off;
- rare events off;
- no strong ascent acceleration sensation;
- no dramatic FOV effect;
- Life Map continuity resolves primarily through depth/opacity substitution.

No flashes, strobing, synchronized twinkle or full-frame luminance pulses. Visual sky targeting cannot be the only navigation method. Low-vision readability must protect Orb silhouette and Ground/sky separation.

## Performance strategy

The atmosphere should remain GPU-cheap relative to the rest of Home. Quality reduction order is cloud integration/sample cost, internal volumetric resolution, particles, high-frequency erosion, then atmospheric sample precision. Do not remove depth hierarchy, horizon haze, primary cloud morphology, star classes or Home→Life Map continuity.

Desktop targets 60 fps where supported. Modern mobile must hold a stable 30 minimum and preserve the same composition rather than fall back to a flat gradient. XR techniques must remain stereo-compatible.
