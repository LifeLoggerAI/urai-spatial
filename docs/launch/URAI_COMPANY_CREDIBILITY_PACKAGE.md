# URAI Company Credibility Package

## Founder Story Framework

Structure:

- origin of the idea
- problem being explored
- engineering journey
- lessons learned
- mission

## Advisor Page Structure

Include:

- advisor role
- expertise
- contribution
- relationship to mission
- verified biography

## Foundation Structure

Sections:

- mission
- research direction
- community purpose
- governance
- transparency

## Press Kit

Include:

- company overview
- founder biography
- product summary
- approved screenshots
- contact information
- verification materials

## FAQ

Topics:

- what is URAI?
- who is it for?
- how does privacy work?
- what exists today?
- what is future roadmap?

## Credibility Rule

Every statement should be tied to a working feature, documentation, evidence, or clearly labeled future direction.
