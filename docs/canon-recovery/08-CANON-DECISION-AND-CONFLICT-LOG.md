Decision: narrow public-beta scope limits present-tense claims but does not delete future canon.

## DEC-003 — Spatial flow reconciliation

- phase chain: Home -> Ascent -> LifeMap -> Focus -> Replay

## DEC-004 — Life Map visual identity

Decision: Life Map is a layered navigable personal galaxy/world, not a flat dashboard. Historical UI that conflicts with that is not visual authority.

## DEC-005 — Focus and Replay

Decision: Focus is approach/arrival into a selected memory/star; Replay is cinematic reconstruction. Text-heavy generic dashboard substitutions are rejected as canon replacements.

## DEC-006 — Privacy-first memory claims

Decision: canon may include persistent personal memory, but current product claims require ownership, consent, export, delete, revocation, retention, and runtime evidence.

## DEC-007 — Future simulation

Decision: future paths are scenarios, not predictions. UX/copy must preserve uncertainty and user editability.

## DEC-008 — Sensitive/Shadow material

Decision: sensitive material cannot be forced open. Entry, disclosure, sensory intensity, and exit remain user-controlled.

## DEC-011 — Legal/entity authority

## DEC-012 — AI role

Decision: UrAi may surface patterns and suggestions with evidence and uncertainty. It does not become the user's authority, therapist, diagnostician, or decision-maker.
