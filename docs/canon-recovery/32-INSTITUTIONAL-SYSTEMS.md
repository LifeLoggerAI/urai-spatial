# URAI Institutional and Operational Systems Canon

Status: SOURCE-BACKED BASELINE

## URAI Labs LLC
Operating/commercial organization and product-development authority where supported by verified corporate records. Repository content does not itself prove legal ownership.

## URAI IP Holdings LLC
Intended IP holding/licensing boundary subject to controlling executed instruments. Do not infer assignment from repo naming.

## URAI Foundation
Public-benefit organization with separate governance. Product, Labs and Foundation authority must not be collapsed.

Foundation surfaces include public information, grants/community programs where actually approved, donations, advisors/professional councils, employee/internal access where implemented, and public-interest education/research work.

## Admin
Internal operations/Council control plane. It must not become a raw consumer-data browser.

## Analytics
Service-to-service analytics subsystem. Raw journals, transcripts, precise location, private relationship content and health records are prohibited inputs to the current analytics contract.

## Communications
Provider capture/delivery and call-intelligence system with tenant, consent, quiet-hours, opt-out, audit and provider boundaries.

## Content
Canonical reusable content/copy/localization/accessibility metadata and publication/provenance packages. Private memories stay in privacy-governed personal-data services.

## Studio
Creative/admin production environment for cinematic/media/story/export workflows and system diagnostics.

## Asset Factory
Generation/validation/provenance/package/publish pipeline. Asset creation does not equal promotion.

## Jobs
Canonical current role: internal asynchronous execution fabric. It is not the public careers marketplace.

## Marketing
Public launch/acquisition copy and campaign system. Claims must stay inside evidence.

## Investors
Public/gated investor narrative and diligence surface. No unsupported traction, legal, security or financial claims.

## B2B
Partner portal. It consumes privacy-reviewed summaries/status, not raw consumer life telemetry.

## Storytime
Private adult/guardian-operated story creation/reading system with age-aware safety, explicit consent, sharing/data-rights boundaries and provider gates.

## RuAI
Separate governed clinician/research/authorized-data portal. It does not grant unrestricted memory access or medical authority.

## Institutional design principle

Institutional systems preserve continuity, governance, operations, public benefit, evidence and authorized collaboration around the personal product. They must not silently expand access to private personal data.
