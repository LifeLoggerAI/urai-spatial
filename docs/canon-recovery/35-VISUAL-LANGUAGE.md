# URAI Visual Language

Status: SOURCE-BACKED CANON BASELINE

## Core aesthetic

Authored, natural, atmospheric, materially rich, emotionally coherent, cinematic, restrained and production-quality.

Avoid cheap neon, generic sci-fi panels, toy-like procedural geometry dominating the image, swirly/cartoon spectacle, floating-island fantasy without source authority, RPG loot glow, and marketing-board layouts pretending to be runtime references.

## Spatial principles

- readable depth;
- human-scale anchoring where embodied;
- stable horizons/landmarks;
- lighting as navigation and emotional support;
- fog/weather for depth and state, never concealment of product weakness;
- particles/fields as secondary living-world language, not noise;
- portals only where current canon actually owns them;
- bodyless conventional first-person unless XR explicitly supports tracked embodiment.

## Material language

Recovered Life Map direction includes reflective dark glass/stone, moonlit metal, holographic glass, restrained volumetric plasma, star-grain particles and warm luminous materials for earned/repair states.

Realm-specific authority overrides generic suggestions.

## Visual truth

- sample data visibly disclosed;
- missing data looks missing/quiet, not fabricated;
- evidence, interpretation and unknown are visually distinct;
- personal weather cannot masquerade as global aggregate truth;
- reconstruction cannot masquerade as recorded evidence;
- final visual acceptance is literal-pixel and exact-head bound.
