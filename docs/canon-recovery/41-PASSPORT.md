# URAI Passport Canon

Status: CURRENT PRODUCT / TWO DISTINCT SURFACES

## Role

Passport represents identity, permissions, context, ownership and continuity boundaries.

## Two distinct canonical expressions

1. Conventional `/passport` UI.
2. Physical Home Passport world-space artifact/reference pack.

These are related but not interchangeable.

## Responsibilities

- show/control permission context;
- expose privacy/ownership state;
- support shareability boundaries;
- represent account/context continuity;
- make revocation/change understandable;
- preserve accessible direct controls.

## Privacy levels in historical Genesis lineage

- localOnly
- privateCloud
- passportShareable

No data moves to shareable merely because a feature requests it.
