# URAI Life Map Canon

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT CONCEPT / CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION SUBSET

## Definition

The Life Map is a private, navigable personal spatial intelligence environment for memory, identity, relationships, goals, habits, decisions, emotional patterns and future scenarios.

It is not a flat dashboard, calendar, photo library, habit tracker, CRM, therapy app, generic AI chat or fake solar system.

## Scale

- Cosmic overview
- Regional
- Intimate

## Core representations

- life-core / continuity;
- chapter rings/regions;
- Memory Stars/artifacts;
- relationship constellations;
- goals/future structures;
- habits/loops;
- decisions/forks;
- achievements/monuments;
- sensitive/private vaults;
- emotional weather;
- future scenarios.

## Navigation

Orbit, glide, semantic zoom, timeline scrub, portal/search jump where authoritative, focus, overview, future-scenario entry, and future walk/XR modes.

## Anti-disorientation

Home return, breadcrumbs/location label, auto-framing, safe camera zones, bounded easing, reduced-motion mode, no uncontrolled spinning and no sudden scale jumps.

## AI role

Suggest connections with evidence and uncertainty; invite correction; never claim unquestionable personal truth.

## Implementation boundary

Today's launch runtime implements only a subset of the full Life Map canon. Missing layers remain roadmap/future rather than deleted.
