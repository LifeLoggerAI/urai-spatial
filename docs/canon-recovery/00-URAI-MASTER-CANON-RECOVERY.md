## Non-negotiable product principles recovered so far

- UrAi is a system-of-systems, not a generic dashboard.
- Passive signals become an explorable personal world.
- Spatial embodiment, camera movement, lighting, depth, terrain/environment, particles/fields, portals, and atmospheric weather are product language rather than decoration where the canon calls for them.
- Life Map is a layered personal galaxy/world, not a flat analytics screen.
- Focus is arrival/approach into a selected memory or star.
- Replay is cinematic.
- Privacy and user control are foundational.
- Accessibility is first-class.
- AI suggestions must preserve evidence, uncertainty, correction, and user control.
- Sensitive/trauma-adjacent material must not be forced open.
- Future AR/VR/XR remains part of the canon even when not launch scope.
- Current launch scope must not erase broader canon.

## Accessibility canon currently established

Support includes explicit design intent for:
- blind users: TTS and directional audio;
- Deaf/hard-of-hearing users: visual/lip-read support and haptics where applicable;
- ADHD/neurodivergent users: reduced stimulation and cognitive-load controls;
- PTSD/TBI-sensitive use: reduced intensity, safer shadow/sensitive overlays, no forced exposure;
- reduced-motion alternatives across spatial transitions.

Exact implementation coverage remains to be mapped per realm.

## Passive-signal canon currently established

Approved categories include:
- voice tone and transcript
- ambient tags
- facial/emotional color signals where explicitly permitted
- steps and motion
- GPS/location snapshots
- stillness
- late-night scrolling
- quick-cancel taps / interaction behavior

Each requires consent, privacy, storage, inference-boundary, and deletion/export treatment before current-product claims.
