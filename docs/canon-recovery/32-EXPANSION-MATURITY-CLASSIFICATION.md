# URAI Expansion Maturity Classification

Status: SOURCE-RECOVERED / CONSERVATIVE

## Professional / therapist

Evidence: product/pricing lineages, professional governance material, RuAI governance, privacy boundaries, investor/product references.

Classification: FUTURE CANON / GOVERNED PROFESSIONAL SURFACE.

Rules:
- does not inherit unrestricted consumer-memory access;
- requires explicit consent and role scope;
- no diagnosis/treatment/medical-device/HIPAA claim unless separately established;
- current professional naming/surface should remain separated from consumer UrAi where governance requires it.

## Family / intergenerational

Evidence: Legacy, private life-reference archive, Storytime, Foundation, family model docs.

Classification: STRONG PLANNED DIRECTION / FUTURE CANON.

Likely surviving themes:
- intergenerational memory continuity;
- family history and durable archive;
- family-safe story creation;
- relationship/lineage evidence.

Unresolved:
- multi-party consent;
- posthumous/inheritance authority;
- shared-memory ownership;
- child/guardian model outside Storytime.

## Education

Evidence: Foundation dossiers, advisor/professional-council material, Foundation site/content.

Classification: INSTITUTIONAL FUTURE / PUBLIC-INTEREST PROGRAM DIRECTION.

Not current consumer-product launch scope. Educational programming must remain distinct from claims of clinical or therapeutic efficacy.

## Creator

Evidence: creator routes/submission APIs, Studio, Content, media/cinema systems.

Classification: PARTIAL CURRENT ECOSYSTEM / FUTURE PRODUCTIZATION.

Creator workflows exist in ecosystem source, but a broad public creator marketplace is not proven as current committed product canon. Treat creator intake/production tooling as existing lineage and marketplace-scale expansion as UNRESOLVED/FUTURE.

## Marketplace

Evidence: early generic UrAi roadmap plus later content/creator/business references.

Classification: HISTORICAL / UNRESOLVED FUTURE.

The early generic Marketplace/Forum/Admin roadmap is superseded as core product authority. Any future marketplace must be redefined under current privacy, provenance, rights, payment, moderation and system-of-systems rules.

## Developer / platform

Evidence: developer-guide/handoff material, EWI v1, content/asset/studio contracts, system-of-systems architecture.

Classification: STRONG PLATFORM DIRECTION / FUTURE CANON.

Surviving platform themes:

- integration registry;
- provider-neutral EWI;
- content/assets/jobs APIs;
- explicit permissions and data classifications;
- no direct secret/provider coupling to core intelligence.

A public third-party developer platform/API product requires separate current launch/commercial authority.

## Wearables / hardware

Evidence: EWI device bridge, wearable federation, haptics authority, future device references.

Classification: FUTURE CANON.

Surviving device classes:
- phone/watch native bridges;
- wearables;
- XR/spatial devices;
- haptics;
- peripheral sensors;

No hardware manufacturing commitment is established by this recovery. Treat dedicated UrAi-branded hardware as RESEARCH/UNRESOLVED unless later authority proves otherwise.

## AI / agents

Evidence: Companion/Narrator, EWI ActionRequest policy, system-of-systems automation, Jobs, Studio, Content.

Classification: CURRENT FOUNDATION + FUTURE CANON.

Current-safe role:
- interpret;
- summarize;
- suggest;
- surface evidence;
- propose actions.

External actions require:
- explicit ActionRequest;
- grant;
- purpose/capability match;
- action class;
- confirmation where required;
- domain adapter;
- audit/receipt.

Fully autonomous consequential action is not a default product right.

## Research / clinical

Evidence: Labs/Foundation/professional governance material.

Classification: RESEARCH / INSTITUTIONAL.

Research concepts must not leak into consumer-product claims without evidence, consent, legal review, and an explicit product decision.

## Maturity rule

A concept can be historically important without being current:
- LOCKED FUTURE CANON = repeatedly established and compatible with later authority;
- STRONG PLANNED DIRECTION = repeated but not yet fully bounded;
- EXPLORED CONCEPT = documented but not re-promoted;
- UNRESOLVED OPTION = still needs explicit authority;
- REJECTED/SUPERSEDED = later canon says not to use as current truth.
