## Minimum-sufficient-artifact rule

A visually significant experiential realm requires at minimum:
1. canonical purpose;
2. state/flow specification;
3. visual/component reference pack;
4. interaction and camera specification;
5. copy requirements;
6. accessibility and sensory-safe behavior;
7. privacy/truth boundary;
8. technical owner/route;
9. tests/proof;
10. roadmap/release classification.

A backend/control-plane system may legitimately mark storyboard or reference art NOT REQUIRED, but it still needs architecture, contracts, privacy/security, operational runbook, and acceptance evidence.
