
## II. Core Principles of Storytime

1.  **Consent at Every Step:** Users must consent to sharing specific memories, consent to who they share them with, and consent to how they are used in a narrative.
2.  **Anonymity as Default:** All memories imported into a shared story are anonymized and abstracted by default. Personal identifiers are removed, and the memory is represented as an archetypal event.
3.  **Narrative, Not History:** Storytime is for creating stories *inspired by* memories, not for creating a factual historical record of a user's life. The goal is shared narrative, not shared surveillance.
4.  **Ephemeral by Design:** Shared stories are not permanent records unless explicitly saved by the participants. The default state is a transient, shared experience.
5.  **No Performance:** Like its sibling systems, Storytime contains no leaderboards, scores, or metrics. The value is in the act of sharing and creating, not in the performance of it.

## III. Forbidden Mechanisms

URAI-Storytime is bound by the same ethical constraints as the core URAI system. It will never include:
- Advertising or data monetization.
- Persuasive technology or behavioral nudging.
- Growth hooks or system-initiated notifications.
- Forced or unskippable interactions.

---

This system allows for collaborative meaning-making, but only after personal meaning has been respected. It turns the private landscape of memory into a shared campfire for stories, with the user's explicit and continuous consent as the only fuel.
