
# URAI: Your Life, A World to Explore.

---

## The Problem: Your life is more than a feed.

Our digital lives are flat, scattered, and impersonal. We create and consume memories through endless scrolling, but we lose the context, the emotion, and the space we were in.

**We have a timeline of our life, but not the world of it.**

---

## The Solution: A Spatial Engine for Your Life

**URAI** turns your personal history into a navigable, cinematic, and deeply private world in AR & VR.

*   **Walk through your memories** like rooms.
*   **See your ideas** as constellations.
*   **Explore the architecture** of your own mind.

> It's not an app you tap. It's a place you visit.

---

## The How: Labs + Foundation

A unique dual structure to build a new kind of digital existence:

| **URAI Labs (For-Profit)**      | **URAI Foundation (Non-Profit)**        |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Builds the **Spatial Engine**     | Guards the **Ethical Mission**          |
| Architects the **Cinematic Runtime** | Upholds **Digital Human Rights**        |
| Focuses on **Technology**       | Focuses on **People & Purpose**         |

**This ensures URAI is built for people, not for profit. Private by default.**
