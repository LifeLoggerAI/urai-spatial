# Rejected UrAi / RuAi Visual Alternatives

Status: provisional rejection record
Controlling issue: `LifeLoggerAI/urai-spatial#1014`

The following alternatives are rejected for the launch master unless later objective evidence justifies reopening them.

## Unrelated new symbol

Rejected because it would sever deterministic lineage from the existing ring-and-orb source system and create unnecessary trademark, QA, derivative, and migration risk.

## Text-only wordmark

Rejected as the sole master because it performs poorly as a favicon, launcher, notification mark, avatar, loading mark, and spatial companion identifier.

## Detailed brain, face, human silhouette, or anatomical symbol

Rejected because detail collapses at small sizes, may imply medical or diagnostic authority, and conflicts with the existing Orb-centered product interaction.

## Gradient-only Orb

Rejected because recognition and state separation cannot depend only on color or gradient rendering. The mark must survive monochrome and forced-colors presentation.

## Different geometric origins for UrAi and RuAi

Rejected because the products require a clear governed family relationship. RuAi must remain distinct through wordmark, restrained evidence-line treatment, and system tone rather than an unrelated logo.

## Fixed uppercase `URAI` consumer wordmark

Rejected for public consumer use because governed capitalization is `UrAi`. Uppercase identifiers may remain only where historically, technically, or legally required.

## Text inside small icons

Rejected for favicon, notification, and small launcher variants because the text is unreadable and creates inconsistent raster artifacts.

## Photographic or rendered 3D Orb as the sole identity

Rejected as the master because it is not deterministic across rendering pipelines, does not reproduce reliably in monochrome, and creates excessive platform variation. Spatial renders may support campaigns but must derive from the flat master.

## Unverified third-party fonts

Rejected until license evidence exists. The governed system stack remains authoritative.

## Production admission

This rejection record does not itself authorize publication. Final derivative admission requires exact-head generation, manifest hashes, visual QA, and the protected production boundary governed by issue #999.
