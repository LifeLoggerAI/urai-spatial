## Public names

- **UrAi** — consumer product.
- **RuAi** — clinician, researcher, and authorized data portal.
- **URAI Labs** — operating company.
- **URAI Foundation** — mission and public-benefit organization.
- **URAI IP Holdings** — intellectual-property owner. Ownership and licensing claims remain conditional on executed legal instruments.

The exact public product spellings are `UrAi` and `RuAi`. Do not publish `URAI`, `Urai`, `UrAI`, `LifeLogger`, `Life Logger`, or `Life OS` as the consumer or portal product name unless the occurrence is explicitly historical, technical, or legal.

## Positioning

UrAi is a private, passive life-replay companion that turns permitted signals into memories, patterns, and short replay experiences. It is not represented as a medical device, therapist, diagnostic system, emergency service, guaranteed prediction system, or replacement for professional care.

RuAi is a separate authorized portal for consented research, clinician, and data workflows. It must not imply unrestricted access to consumer data or automatic clinical interpretation.

## Claim-safe language

Approved foundation statements:

- Private by design.
- User-controlled data and consent.
- Passive life replay with explicit permission boundaries.
- Accessibility-aware experiences.
- Veteran-led operating company, when factually attributed to URAI Labs.
- Preview, beta, or experimental labels where certification is incomplete.

Prohibited without separate evidence and approval:

- FDA approved, clinically proven, HIPAA compliant, SOC 2 certified, end-to-end encrypted, anonymous, unhackable, medically accurate, diagnostic, therapeutic, guaranteed, patent pending, patented, world first, or production live.
- Named partnerships, customer counts, revenue, valuation, traction, or deployment claims without retained evidence.
- Claims that URAI IP Holdings owns specific rights unless signed assignment or license evidence is retained.

## Visual relationship

UrAi and RuAi share the same geometric origin and spacing system. UrAi is expressive, warm, spatial, and companion-led. RuAi is restrained, evidence-led, professional, and clearly separated from consumer memory experiences.

No new logo is production-authoritative until it is traced to the strongest existing approved master, receives visual approval, is entered in the governed asset manifest, and passes derivative validation.

Display fallback stack:

`Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif`

## Spacing and clear space

The base spacing unit is 4 px. Logo clear space is one orb-radius around every side of the full lockup. Minimum sizes remain provisional until the existing master mark is measured and approved:

- Full lockup: 96 px wide minimum for screen use.
- Compact lockup: 48 px wide minimum.
- Orb-only mark: 24 px minimum; use a simplified small-size derivative below 32 px.

## Accessibility

- Text and essential controls must meet WCAG AA contrast.
- Brand gradients must never be the sole carrier of state.
- Every motion asset requires a reduced-motion equivalent.
- Every meaningful sound requires a visual or haptic equivalent.
- Forced-colors mode must preserve structure and labels.

## Governance

Every production candidate must record stable ID, filename, dimensions or duration, media type, byte size, SHA-256, source, provenance, rights status, sensitivity, route/component, accessibility equivalent, optimization, approval state, deployment state, rollback identity, version, and replacement lineage.

Experimental V2-V5 assets must remain isolated from certified V1 runtime paths until separately validated.
