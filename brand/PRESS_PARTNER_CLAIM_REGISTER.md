# UrAi Press, Partner, and Public Claim Register

Status: controlled copy authority. This file does not itself approve a claim; it records the evidence and approval required before public use.

## Approved identity descriptions

### Short

UrAi is a permission-bound companion for remembering, reflecting on, and revisiting meaningful moments.

### Medium

UrAi is a private, permission-bound life-replay companion designed to help people remember, reflect on, and reconnect with meaningful moments. RuAi is the separate authorized portal for clinician, researcher, and governed data workflows within granted permissions.

### Organization relationship

- **UrAi** — consumer product.
- **RuAi** — authorized clinician, researcher, and data portal.
- **URAI Labs** — operating company.
- **URAI Foundation** — mission and public-benefit organization.
- **URAI IP Holdings** — intellectual-property owner.

These descriptions must remain consistent across press, crowdfunding, investor, partner, store, website, and social materials.

## Evidence-required claims

The following require retained current evidence and named approval before use:

| Claim category | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Number of users, revenue, growth, retention, waitlist, or engagement | Current dated analytics or financial authority |
| Production availability, launch geography, or `now live` | Protected issue #999 terminal proof and public fingerprint parity |
| Encryption or security architecture | Current implemented architecture, independent verification where claimed, and security approval |
| Privacy or anonymity | Exact data flows, threat model, deletion and consent behavior, and legal review |
| Accessibility conformance | Scope-specific audit evidence; automated scans alone are insufficient |
| Clinical outcomes or medical benefit | Appropriate clinical evidence and regulatory/legal review |
| HIPAA, GDPR, COPPA, SOC 2, ISO, or similar compliance | Formal scoped legal/compliance authority and current evidence |
| Patent, patent-pending, trademark, or exclusivity | Counsel-confirmed filing or registration records |
| Partnerships, integrations, endorsements, or customers | Executed agreement or written authorization |
| User payments, revenue share, or earnings | Implemented terms, eligibility, economics, payment operation, and legal review |
| Market leadership, first, only, category ownership, or superiority | Defensible current comparative evidence and legal review |

## Prohibited unsupported wording

Do not publish any of the following without the required evidence and approval:

- Diagnoses, predicts, prevents, treats, or cures a medical or mental-health condition.
- Prevents suicide, relapse, PTSD, dementia, disease, or harm.
- Fully anonymous, completely private, impossible to breach, or zero risk.
- HIPAA compliant, clinically proven, medically validated, or doctor approved.
- End-to-end encrypted unless the exact full data path supports that statement.
- Pays every user or guarantees income.
- Now live globally.
- Patented, patent pending, trademarked, or protected by a stated filing count without counsel records.
- Officially partnered with or endorsed by the VA, Department of Defense, military, hospital, university, platform, or named company without written authority.

## Steward and founder story boundary

The initial launch story may remain product-led and does not require a face. Personal military, disability, trauma, medical, legal, or family details are private by default and require explicit scope approval before publication. Public copy should not turn personal experience into a claim of clinical efficacy.

## Partner and investor boundary

Confidential materials may include forecasts, roadmap, invention-capture materials, and future V2-V5 concepts only when clearly labeled as forecasts, planned work, experimental work, or counsel-prep material. They must not be presented as shipped production capability.

## Approval record

Every public artifact must record:

- artifact ID and version;
- intended channel and audience;
- exact claims used;
- evidence references;
- legal, brand, privacy, security, accessibility, and clinical reviewers where applicable;
- approval date and expiration or re-review date;
- locale and native-review status;
- public release status;
- rollback or replacement identity.
