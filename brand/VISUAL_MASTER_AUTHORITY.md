## Selected geometric origin

The selected public identity origin is the existing deterministic ring-and-orb system already implemented in:

- `urai-tier1/src/brand/urai-brand.registry.ts`
- `urai-tier1/scripts/generate-urai-brand-svgs.mjs`
- `urai-tier1/brand/exports/svg/uraiCore.light.svg`

This selection preserves existing source lineage and avoids inventing a disconnected mark. The visual origin is a circular boundary enclosing a central Orb. The circle communicates a protected personal world; the Orb communicates the companion and intelligence presence.

## Public identities

- **UrAi** — consumer product. Expressive, warm, spatial, companion-led.
- **RuAi** — authorized clinician, researcher, and data portal. Restrained, evidence-led, and visibly distinct from the consumer memory experience.
- **URAI Labs** — operating company.
- **URAI Foundation** — mission and public-benefit organization.
- **URAI IP Holdings** — intellectual-property entity; ownership claims remain conditional on executed instruments.

Required capitalization is exact. Legacy `URAI` ecosystem identifiers may remain in technical compatibility paths but must not replace the public `UrAi` or `RuAi` names.

## Master forms

### UrAi primary lockup

Ring-and-orb symbol followed by the wordmark `UrAi`. Use on product headers, web hero surfaces, press masters, and wide social layouts.

### UrAi compact lockup

Ring-and-orb symbol above or beside a compact `UrAi` wordmark. Use where the primary lockup cannot maintain minimum size.

### Orb-only mark

The ring-and-orb symbol without text. Use for favicons, launchers, avatars, splash surfaces, loading surfaces, and other small or shape-constrained applications.

### RuAi primary lockup

The shared ring-and-orb origin followed by `RuAi`, with a restrained evidence-line modifier that remains recognizable without color. The modifier must not imply clinical certification or unrestricted data access.

### Monochrome treatment

Use a single foreground color with no gradient dependency. The central Orb and outer ring must remain distinguishable through geometry or negative space.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Clear space: at least one central-Orb radius on every side of the complete lockup.
- Primary lockup: 96 px minimum width for screen use.
- Compact lockup: 48 px minimum width.
- Orb-only mark: 24 px minimum.
- Below 32 px, decorative secondary lines are removed and only the outer boundary plus central Orb remain.
- Notification marks contain no text.

## Light and dark masters

Light-background masters use dark ink with the product accent on the Orb. Dark-background masters use paper-white geometry with the product accent on the Orb. Backgrounds must preserve WCAG AA contrast for accompanying text and essential UI.

## Accessibility requirements

- Essential identity recognition must survive monochrome presentation.
- Gradients are decorative only.
- Forced-colors mode must retain boundary and center geometry.
- Motion versions require a static equivalent.
- No sound or haptic identity treatment may carry required meaning alone.
