# URAI V1 - Adventure Modules Specification

This document outlines the first three core adventure modules for the URAI V1 application. Each module follows the "blended but tightly scoped" design philosophy: a **Narrative** contains an implicit **Skill**, which is unlocked by a safe, structured **Creative Action**.

---

##  Module 1: The Whispering Woods & The Frightened Fox

*   **Narrative Core:** A small fox is hiding in its den. It's afraid of the "scary whispers" the wind makes as it blows through the woods. The fox feels a shaky feeling and doesn't want to come out and play.

*   **Implicit Skill:** Emotional Naming & Externalization. Helping the child understand that an abstract fear can be seen, named, and made less intimidating. It separates the feeling from the self.

*   **Creative Action & Flow:**
    1.  **Introduction:** The Companion introduces the user to the fox, explaining the situation in simple, physical terms (e.g., "The fox's tummy feels shaky when the wind whispers").
    2.  **Externalization Prompt:** The Companion asks for help: "I wonder what those whispers look like. If we could see them, maybe they wouldn't be so scary. Can you help me draw the whisper-wind?"
    3.  **Creative Input:** The user is presented with a simple, constrained drawing tool (e.g., a single color line tool on a dark canvas). They draw their interpretation of the "whisper-wind."
    4.  **Naming & Containment:** After the drawing is complete, the Companion says, "Look at that! Now that we can see it, it's not so scary. What silly name should we give it?" The user inputs a name. The drawing, now named, is animated shrinking and being placed into a "Story Box" for safekeeping.
    5.  **Resolution:** With the scary sound "contained," the fox peeks out, feels braver, and thanks the user for their help before the module concludes.

## Module 2: The Grumpy Bear Who Lost His Roar

*   **Narrative Core:** A large, usually friendly bear is grumpy and sad. His big, happy ROAR has gone missing, and all that comes out is a quiet, frustrated grumble. He feels stuck and unheard.

*   **Implicit Skill:** Understanding & Expressing Frustration. Connecting an internal emotional state (frustration, sadness) to a physical expression (voice, sound).

*   **Creative Action & Flow:**
    1.  **Introduction:** The Companion explains that the bear is feeling "all bunched up inside" because he can't get his big roar out.
    2.  **Exploration Prompt:** The Companion asks, "Maybe the roar is just sleeping. Let's try to wake it up. Can you show me what a happy roar sounds like?"
    3.  **Creative Input:** The user uses their device's microphone. They are prompted to make a sound. The app visualizes their sound wave in a simple, non-judgmental way on the screen. The Companion encourages them to try different sounds: "Let's try a quiet, rumbling one... now a big, spiky one!"
    4.  **Connection:** After a few tries, the bear on screen reacts positively to one of the roars. The Companion says, "That's it! You helped him remember how it feels!"
    5.  **Resolution:** The bear lets out a big, animated, happy ROAR (a pre-recorded sound effect), shakes its body loose, and gives the user a happy wave. The user's successful "roar shape" is saved into their Story Box.

## Module 3: The Lonely Firefly

*   **Narrative Core:** A single, small firefly is blinking sadly by itself. A larger group of fireflies is nearby, creating a beautiful, pulsing light dance, but the lonely firefly's light is out of sync, so it can't join in.

*   **Implicit Skill:** Social Attunement & Rhythmic Co-regulation. Learning the feeling of being "out of sync" with a group and the joy of finding the rhythm to rejoin it.

*   **Creative Action & Flow:**
    1.  **Introduction:** The Companion points out the lonely firefly and explains, "It wants to join the dance, but it's forgotten the rhythm."
    2.  **Observation Prompt:** The user is shown the pattern of the main group of fireflies blinking in a steady, calm rhythm. "Let's watch the dance for a moment and feel the beat."
    3.  **Creative Input:** A large, simple button appears on screen. The Companion says, "Can you tap this button in the same rhythm? Let's help our little friend remember the beat." The user taps along. The app provides gentle visual feedback, showing the user's taps aligning with the group's pulses.
    4.  **Synchronization:** As the user successfully matches the rhythm for a few beats, the lonely firefly on screen starts to blink in sync with them.
    5.  **Resolution:** Once synchronized, the firefly joyfully flies over and joins the larger group, merging into their beautiful, pulsing dance. The module ends with the complete, harmonious scene. A small, glowing "rhythm star" is added to the user's Story Box.
