# URAI Intellectual-Property Chain-of-Title Readiness

Status: evidence and remediation authority; this document does not create, assign, file, register, or perfect legal rights

## Purpose

Maintain a complete evidence-backed inventory of ecosystem intellectual property and prevent unsupported ownership, patent, trademark, exclusivity, or licensing claims.

## Inventory classes

The chain-of-title register must cover:

1. source code, build systems, tests, infrastructure, schemas, and documentation;
2. UrAi, RuAi, URAI Labs, URAI Foundation, and URAI IP Holdings names and marks;
3. domains, social handles, store listings, and account custody;
4. visual assets, models, textures, animations, icons, and derivative masters;
5. audio, music, sound effects, voice assets, captions, and transcripts;
6. prompts, model configurations, evaluation sets, and generation receipts;
7. datasets, taxonomies, schemas, synthetic data, and transformation methods;
8. inventions, technical methods, algorithms, workflows, and invention disclosures;
9. research, product, governance, operational, marketing, and legal-preparation documents;
10. open-source, commercial, provider, font, media, and platform dependencies;
11. employee, contractor, advisor, collaborator, and contributor materials.

## Required evidence per item

Each item must record:

- stable asset or invention ID;
- title and description;
- creator or contributor;
- creation date and source location;
- repository commit, file hash, provider request ID, or other provenance;
- original, derivative, commissioned, licensed, open-source, or user-owned classification;
- applicable agreement or license;
- restrictions, attribution, copyleft, field-of-use, territory, and term;
- current custodian;
- intended owner;
- executed assignment or license evidence;
- trademark, patent, copyright, or trade-secret treatment where applicable;
- public disclosure dates;
- approval and review state;
- replacement and retirement lineage.

## User-data boundary

User memories, recordings, relationships, locations, health-adjacent inputs, personal data, and user-created content are not transferred to URAI IP Holdings merely because they interact with UrAi. Product terms, consent, and applicable law govern limited processing rights separately.

## Contributor chain of title

Before relying on exclusive ownership, retain applicable:

- employment invention assignment;
- contractor work-made-for-hire and assignment language;
- contributor license or assignment;
- confidentiality and trade-secret obligations;
- moral-rights treatment where permitted;
- pre-existing materials schedule;
- third-party dependency disclosure;
- signature, date, parties, and authority.

An NDA alone does not assign intellectual property.

## Open-source compliance

For every distributed dependency, record:

- package and version;
- license and notice requirements;
- source and lockfile identity;
- modification status;
- distribution context;
- attribution or source-offer obligations;
- incompatibility review where relevant;
- security and maintenance status.

No dependency is assumed permissive merely because it is publicly available.

## Generated assets and models

Provider-generated material requires:

- provider and model;
- request date and request ID where available;
- prompt or bounded instruction record;
- account and applicable provider terms;
- source inputs and their rights status;
- output hash and dimensions;
- human modifications;
- similarity, duplication, and restricted-content review;
- admission decision and runtime lineage.

Provider output is not automatically exclusive, registrable, or free of third-party risk.

## Trademark readiness

For each proposed mark, prepare:

- exact mark and owner candidate;
- goods and services scope;
- first-use evidence where truthful;
- domain and handle inventory;
- clearance-search record;
- conflicting uses and risk analysis;
- specimen candidates;
- filing jurisdiction and basis;
- counsel decision;
- filing, office action, registration, renewal, or abandonment receipts.

Do not use `registered`, the registration symbol, or a filing-status claim without matching evidence. Common-law use and a filed or registered mark are distinct states.

## Patent readiness

For each invention candidate, prepare:

- invention title and contributors;
- problem, novelty, implementation, alternatives, and diagrams;
- earliest conception and reduction-to-practice evidence;
- related public disclosures, sales, demonstrations, and repository history;
- known prior art and differentiators;
- ownership and assignment status;
- counsel review and filing decision;
- exact filing receipt if submitted.

`Patent pending` is prohibited until a qualifying filing receipt exists. A draft disclosure or provisional-preparation document is not a filing.

## Domain and account custody

Record registrant, account owner, recovery methods, renewal status, nameservers, transfer lock, billing authority, backup custody, and incident recovery for every domain and platform account. Never store passwords, full payment credentials, identity-verification secrets, or recovery codes in the evidence register.

## Licensing readiness

Every outbound license must identify:

- licensor authority and chain of title;
- licensed assets or rights;
- exclusivity;
- purpose and field of use;
- territory and term;
- sublicensing;
- fees and audit rights;
- security and confidentiality;
- brand controls;
- user-data exclusion;
- improvements and derivatives;
- termination, return, deletion, and transition;
- signatures and effective date.

## Current classification

- Repository source and asset provenance systems: implemented, evidence varies by item.
- Brand visual authority and deterministic derivatives: implemented; exact-current-main evidence remains separately governed.
- Certified V1 asset lineage: governed; do not regenerate or replace without admission evidence.
- URAI IP Holdings ownership: prepared, external execution required.
- Trademark filings or registrations: unverified by this authority.
- Patent filings or patent-pending status: unverified by this authority.
- Contributor assignments and inter-entity licenses: require itemized executed-instrument review.

## Remediation order

1. Freeze the current inventory and provenance.
2. Identify creators, pre-existing materials, and third-party dependencies.
3. Obtain missing contributor and contractor instruments.
4. Execute inter-entity assignments and licenses with qualified counsel.
5. Complete trademark clearance and filing decisions.
6. Complete invention disclosures and filing decisions before avoidable public disclosure.
7. Retain filing, signature, payment, renewal, and custody receipts.
8. Update public claims only after evidence exists.
