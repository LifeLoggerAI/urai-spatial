# URAI Organizational Operating Boundaries

Status: execution-ready governance preparation; external legal formation, filings, signatures, and officer authority are not created by this document

## Ecosystem identities

- **UrAi** — consumer product and personal experience.
- **RuAi** — separate governed professional and authorized-data platform.
- **URAI Labs** — operating and product organization.
- **URAI Foundation** — mission and public-benefit organization.
- **URAI IP Holdings** — intended intellectual-property ownership and licensing organization, subject to executed instruments.

Product names, organizational names, responsibilities, and public claims must remain separate. Technical repository history using `URAI` may remain where changing it would damage lineage.

## URAI Labs

URAI Labs owns day-to-day product execution unless a signed instrument assigns a specific responsibility elsewhere. Its prepared responsibilities include:

- product and engineering operations;
- privacy, security, accessibility, and release operations;
- vendor and provider administration;
- support and incident response;
- employment and contractor administration;
- product communications and approved commercial operations;
- maintaining evidence and operational receipts.

URAI Labs must not claim ownership of an asset assigned elsewhere, charitable status, clinical authority, a partnership, or a certification without evidence.

## URAI Foundation

The Foundation mission surface may prepare public-benefit programs, accessibility initiatives, education, research-support concepts, grants, and community work. Until formation and program approvals are retained:

- charitable or tax-exempt status is not claimed;
- donations are not solicited as tax deductible;
- grants, councils, advisors, programs, and public commitments are not represented as operational solely because draft materials exist;
- product customer funds and Foundation funds remain separately accounted;
- access to UrAi or RuAi data is not granted by mission status.

Confirmed human participation may be described only to the exact extent supported by written evidence and publication consent.

## URAI IP Holdings

URAI IP Holdings is the intended long-term owner or licensor of selected ecosystem intellectual property. Until executed assignments and licenses exist, references must remain conditional.

Prepared responsibilities include:

- maintaining the IP inventory and chain-of-title register;
- receiving executed assignments;
- licensing approved rights to URAI Labs and approved third parties;
- preserving provenance and contributor records;
- coordinating trademark, patent, copyright, and domain strategy with qualified counsel;
- preventing licensing terms from overriding user data rights or consent.

URAI IP Holdings does not own user memories, personal data, or consent merely because it owns software or brand assets.

## Product separation

UrAi and RuAi must have distinct:

- audiences and product purposes;
- authentication and authorization boundaries;
- privacy notices and consent scopes;
- public claims and visual tone;
- data access policies;
- audit and revocation behavior;
- release and incident receipts.

A shared brand family does not imply shared unrestricted data access.

## Decision authority

Until signed corporate governance instruments supersede this preparation, every material action must identify the actual authorized human or external authority. Required named authority categories include:

- product and engineering release;
- privacy and data protection;
- security and incident response;
- accessibility acceptance;
- finance and material spending;
- legal and regulatory review;
- IP assignment and licensing;
- Foundation programs and public-benefit commitments;
- public claims and partnership announcements.

A repository role, document author, automated agent, or workflow cannot manufacture corporate officer, legal, fiduciary, or regulatory authority.

## Escalation path

1. Protect people, private data, credentials, and production integrity.
2. Stop or contain the affected operation.
3. Record exact environment, actor, timestamp, evidence, and scope.
4. Notify the responsible operational authority.
5. Escalate legal, privacy, security, financial, accessibility, or clinical questions to the appropriate qualified reviewer.
6. Preserve rollback and communication receipts.
7. Resume only after the named acceptance condition is met.

## Stewardship and succession preparation

A future executed succession instrument should define:

- triggering conditions;
- interim operational authority;
- protection of user rights and mission constraints;
- custody of credentials, repositories, domains, legal records, and evidence;
- board, trustee, manager, or officer authority as applicable;
- conflicts of interest;
- emergency continuity;
- revocation and replacement;
- public communication authority.

No successor, board, officer, trustee, or transfer is created by this document.

## Inter-entity agreements required before formal reliance

- IP assignment and license agreements;
- trademark and domain license or custody agreements;
- shared-services agreement where appropriate;
- data-processing and security responsibilities;
- Foundation/Labs independence and conflict policy;
- contributor and contractor invention-assignment agreements;
- funding, reimbursement, and cost-allocation terms;
- brand and public-claims license;
- termination, transition, and continuity terms.

## Public claim boundary

Prepared structure may be described as planned or being established. Formation, good standing, tax status, ownership, licensing, advisors, programs, partnerships, and fiduciary authority require retained primary evidence before being described as completed.
