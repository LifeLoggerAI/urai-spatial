# URAI Economic Participation Boundaries

Status: policy and architecture preparation; no payout, revenue-share, marketplace, regulated-data sale, or earnings program is activated by this document

## Purpose

Prepare subscriptions, referrals, institutional licensing, and optional user economic participation without misleading users, bypassing consent, or activating financial and regulated-data flows before the required legal, tax, payment, privacy, and operational controls exist.

## V1 boundary

V1 may support clearly priced product access and nonfinancial referral attribution when technically and legally approved. The following are not required for V1 and remain separately gated:

- user data revenue sharing;
- dataset marketplace operation;
- institutional data purchasing;
- creator or contributor payouts;
- cash-value credits;
- guaranteed referral compensation;
- investment, yield, ownership, or profit-sharing representations.

## Prohibited claims and behavior

- No guaranteed income, minimum earnings, passive-income promise, or investment-return claim.
- No automatic sale, license, or sharing of user data.
- No prechecked or bundled economic-consent toggle.
- No suggestion that longer product use guarantees higher compensation.
- No hidden reduction in service quality for users who decline data participation.
- No sale or license of private memories, exact location, health-adjacent inputs, relationships, recordings, or derived patterns outside the exact approved scope.
- No payout before identity, eligibility, tax, sanctions, fraud, payment, reversal, and support procedures are operational.

## Subscription readiness

A subscription offering requires:

- exact product and entitlement definition;
- price, currency, billing interval, taxes, trial, renewal, cancellation, refund, and downgrade behavior;
- receipt and account history;
- app-store and web-channel consistency where applicable;
- accessibility and localization review of purchase flows;
- protection against duplicate charging;
- entitlement restoration;
- support and dispute handling;
- analytics consent separation;
- current terms and privacy notice;
- protected test and production receipts.

A pricing idea or UI route is not an activated subscription system.

## Referral readiness

Referral operation requires:

- attribution period and qualifying event;
- anti-fraud controls;
- disclosure of incentives;
- self-referral and duplicate-account policy;
- privacy-minimized tracking;
- consent and cookie treatment where required;
- reward type, expiration, reversal, and support rules;
- tax and payment treatment for cash-value rewards;
- accessible terms and account history.

## Optional data participation

A future program must be separate, explicit, granular, revocable, and understandable. A valid participation record includes:

- program and consent-copy version;
- approved data categories and precision;
- prohibited uses;
- approved recipients or recipient class;
- purpose and term;
- compensation method and calculation;
- fees, reserves, adjustments, and timing;
- withdrawal and future-use cutoff;
- downstream deletion or retention behavior;
- tax and eligibility treatment;
- disputes and support;
- audit and receipt access.

Declining or withdrawing must not remove core account rights or obscure deletion and export controls.

## Compensation truth

Before displaying an amount to a user, the system must distinguish:

- estimate;
- accrued but unsettled amount;
- approved payable amount;
- paid amount;
- reversed or withheld amount;
- tax or fee deduction;
- expired or forfeited amount with authority.

Every calculation requires a reproducible ledger and versioned rule. Marketing copy may not convert estimates or hypothetical models into earned income.

## Institutional licensing

An institutional arrangement requires:

- verified contracting parties and authority;
- licensed software, services, datasets, or outputs identified precisely;
- user-data and IP boundaries;
- permitted and prohibited uses;
- security and access requirements;
- consent and lawful-basis responsibilities;
- audit, incident, deletion, return, and termination terms;
- geography, term, fees, taxes, and payment;
- research publication and reidentification restrictions where relevant;
- executed agreement and operational evidence.

A conversation, advisor relationship, pilot idea, or draft term sheet is not a partnership or customer contract.

## Marketplace boundary

Any marketplace requires separate seller/provider verification, listing governance, moderation, licensing, payment, tax, refund, dispute, fraud, privacy, accessibility, and prohibited-content controls. It remains future scope and must not be represented as currently available.

## Accounting and custody

User funds, company revenue, Foundation funds, refunds, reserves, taxes, and provider costs must remain distinguishable. No system should custody user money or represent a balance as cash-equivalent without appropriate legal and payment architecture.

## Activation gates

Economic participation may activate only after retained evidence of:

1. product and eligibility rules;
2. legal, privacy, and tax review;
3. consent and withdrawal behavior;
4. payment-provider approval and restricted credentials;
5. ledger integrity and reconciliation;
6. fraud, sanctions, identity, and abuse controls as applicable;
7. refund, reversal, dispute, and support handling;
8. deletion and downstream propagation;
9. protected-environment tests and rollback;
10. approved public claims.

## Current classification

- Subscription and upgrade concepts: implementation state must be verified per route and payment environment.
- Referral concepts: groundwork exists; financial activation is unverified.
- Institutional licensing: prepared, executed agreements required.
- Optional anonymized participation and user revenue sharing: future scope, not required for V1.
- Marketplace and payout operations: future scope, not production authorized.
