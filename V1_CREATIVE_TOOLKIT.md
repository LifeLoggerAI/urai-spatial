# URAI V1 - Creative Toolkit Specification

## I. Core Philosophy: Constrained Creativity for Safety & Focus

The creative tools in URAI V1 are not a full-featured art suite. They are intentionally limited to guide the user's expression towards the specific emotional and narrative goal of each Adventure Module. The goal is to provide just enough agency to feel empowering, without the complexity that leads to distraction or frustration. Every tool is designed to be simple, non-judgmental, and impossible to "fail" at using.

---

## II. Tool Specifications

### 1. The Drawing Tool ("Whisper-Wind" Module)

*   **Functionality:** A simple line-drawing interface.
*   **Constraints:**
    *   **Single Tool:** One drawing tool is available: a single, continuous line. No brushes, fills, or erasers.
    *   **Single Color:** The line has a fixed, gentle, glowing color (e.g., a soft white or light blue). The user does not choose the color. This prevents choice paralysis and keeps the focus on the form of the line.
    *   **No Eraser:** The lack of an eraser is a deliberate choice. It encourages acceptance of what has been created and prevents the frustration of trying to achieve "perfection." Every line is part of the creation.
    *   **Simple Canvas:** The drawing occurs on a dark, plain background. There are no layers or complex UI elements.
    *   **Input:** The user draws with a single finger touch on the screen.
*   **Purpose:** To capture the *form* and *energy* of a feeling (the "whisper-wind"), not to create a detailed illustration. The simplicity ensures any child can create something and feel successful.

### 2. The Sound Tool ("Grumpy Bear" Module)

*   **Functionality:** A microphone input that generates a simple, real-time visualization of the user's voice.
*   **Constraints:**
    *   **Visualization:** The sound is visualized as a simple, abstract shape or line graph. It is not a literal or technical representation (e.g., no decibel levels or frequency numbers). The shape should be organic and pleasing to look at (e.g., a pulsing, spiky blob or a flowing wave).
    *   **No Recording & Playback (V1):** For safety and privacy, the tool does not record and store the user's voice. It visualizes the sound in real-time, and once the interaction is complete, the audio data is gone. The *shape* of the successful "roar" is what is saved as a visual artifact in the Story Box, not the sound itself.
    *   **Non-Judgmental Feedback:** The visualization does not label the sound as "good" or "bad," "loud" or "quiet." The feedback is purely visual and tied to the narrative. The bear in the story simply reacts positively when a sound of a certain shape is made.
*   **Purpose:** To create a safe, playful link between physical expression (making a sound) and an internal feeling (frustration). It encourages vocal expression without fear of judgment.

### 3. The Rhythm Tool ("Lonely Firefly" Module)

*   **Functionality:** A single, large on-screen button that responds to user taps.
*   **Constraints:**
    *   **Simple Input:** A single tap is the only interaction. There are no swipes, holds, or complex gestures.
    *   **Generous Timing:** The rhythm-matching logic is intentionally forgiving. The goal is to feel the rhythm, not to pass a difficult test. The window for a "successful" tap is wide.
    *   **Visual Feedback:** When the user taps, a visual pulse emanates from the button. When their tap aligns with the firefly group's rhythm, the pulse visually syncs up in a satisfying way (e.g., their pulse ring and the group's pulse ring glow brighter together).
    *   **No Failure State:** The user can never "lose." If they are out of sync, the lonely firefly simply continues to blink on its own. The Companion provides gentle encouragement (e.g., "Let's try to feel the beat together."). There are no penalty sounds, visuals, or messages.
*   **Purpose:** To teach co-regulation and attunement in a low-stakes, meditative way. The focus is on the positive feeling of synchronization, not the fear of failure.
