# 🔐 URAI-SPATIAL V1.0.0 — FOUNDER LOCK DECLARATION

## 1. PREAMBLE

This document serves as the permanent and immutable definition of the core identity, boundaries, and intent of the URAI-SPATIAL V1.0.0 system. It is the foundational source of truth against which all features, architectural changes, and strategic decisions shall be measured. Its purpose is to prevent silent drift and to bind the system to its original mandate.

This is the lock.

---

## 2. CORE IDENTITY DEFINITION

URAI-SPATIAL V1 is classified as a **Reflective Archive**.

This is a hybrid of **Memory Infrastructure** and **Reflective Intelligence System**.

It is defined as:

*   A structured life archive.
*   A reflective visualization engine.
*   A personal data mirror.
*   A pattern detection layer for the purpose of self-reflection.

URAI-SPATIAL **is not** a predictive system, an emotional OS, or a digital surrogate. It represents the past; it does not simulate a future or an autonomous present.

---

## 4. PRIMARY LONG-TERM INTENT

The primary and overriding purpose of URAI-SPATIAL is:

**To help people understand their past through structured, visual reflection.**

Secondary goals, such as pattern analysis or behavioral insight, must exclusively serve this primary intent. If a feature does not enhance a user's ability to reflect on their own verified past, it is out of scope.

---

## 5. RISK TOLERANCE CLASSIFICATION

The risk tolerance for URAI-SPATIAL V1 is **LOW**.

This classification mandates a conservative approach to feature development, prioritizing stability, security, user safety, and deterministic integrity over experimental functionality. Predictive, emotional, or agentic features are classified as High Risk and are therefore excluded from the V1 scope.

---

## 6. ARCHITECTURAL MANDATE

The Core Identity and Hard Red Lines translate into the following architectural mandates:

*   **No Predictive Models:** The architecture will not include frameworks for predictive analytics, mood forecasting, or behavioral simulation.
*   **Data Flow is One-Way:** The system visualizes user data. It does not generate new, derivative data and attribute it to the user's identity model.
*   **Deterministic Core:** The rendering and data processing core must remain deterministic. Integrity and reproducibility are paramount.
*   **Feature Gating:** All new features must be explicitly validated against the Hard Red Lines before integration. The default answer to a feature that approaches a boundary is "no".

---

## 7. GOVERNANCE

This Founder Lock is binding on all contributors, stakeholders, and future development of the V1 branch. Amendments to this lock require a formal, documented, and unanimous review process by a designated governance board.

This document will be committed to the root of the URAI-SPATIAL repository as a permanent artifact.
