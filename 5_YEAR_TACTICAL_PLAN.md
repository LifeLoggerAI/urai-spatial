# URAI: The First 5-Year Tactical Execution Plan

**Overarching Goal:** To successfully execute the "Personal Reflection Era" (Years 1-5), establishing URAI as a trusted, performant, and profound tool for a core group of reflective individuals and therapists. This plan prioritizes integrity over growth and durability over speed.

**Guiding Documents:** This plan is a direct implementation of the `ARCHETYPE_MODEL_SPECIFICATION.md`, executed within the strict ethical constraints of the `PSYCHOLOGICAL_RISK_AUDIT.md` and the design principles of the `URAI-SPATIAL-PROJECT-CHARTER.md`.

---

## **Year 1: Foundation & Core Engine**

*   **Theme:** Build the core technical and ethical foundation in isolation. Prove the engine works.
*   **Team:** Founder-led. A small, fanatically focused team of no more than 3-4 engineers and designers who understand the core principles.
*   **Core Activities:**
    1.  **Engine Implementation:** Build the `Archetype Evolution Engine` as a private-by-default, on-device system. Translate the `ARCHETYPE_MODEL_SPECIFICATION.md` into clean, maintainable code.
    2.  **Visualizer (Life-Map):** Implement the WebXR `LifeMapLayer` as the primary visualization, using the production-grade architecture already defined (instancing, shaders, etc.).
    3.  **Implicit Input:** Develop a minimal, non-intrusive mechanism for capturing the daily `EmotionVector`. This could be a single, reflective prompt or a simple, abstract interface. It must not feel like data entry.
    4.  **Establish Red Team Protocol:** Formalize and operationalize the "Red Team" concept from the risk audit. This team's sole job is to actively try to misuse the system and validate the psychological safety mitigations.
*   **Key Milestones:**
    *   **Q2:** Internal Alpha of the Archetype Engine with a functional `LifeMap` visualizer.
    *   **Q4:** Onboard the first 100 early adopters—a curated group of reflective individuals who are given clear context on the project's intent.
    *   **EOY:** All user data is 100% on-device or in the user's private, developer-mode-enabled cloud storage. Zero server-side data collection.

---

## **Year 2: Refinement & Therapeutic Validation**

*   **Theme:** Refine the engine based on real-world feedback and begin building a bridge to the therapeutic community.
*   **Team:** Augment the core team with a part-time Clinical Advisor to formalize the ethical review process and guide narrative development.
*   **Core Activities:**
    1.  **Model Tuning:** Use anonymized, *opt-in* data from the early adopter cohort to refine and tune the `ArchetypeMatrix` values.
    2.  **Narrative Engine:** Build the "Narrative Humility" language generator for the Companion. It should be based on templates and rules, not a large language model, to ensure its output is always within the bounds of the risk audit.
    3.  **Implement Safety Controls:** Build the "Sanctuary of Silence" and "Interpretive Choice" features defined in the risk audit.
*   **Key Milestones:**
    *   **Q2:** Present a non-public version of URAI to a select advisory group of 5-10 therapists for deep feedback.
    *   **Q3:** Begin drafting the **IRB & Research Ethics Protocol** based on the real-world usage patterns and feedback from the advisory group.
    *   **Q4:** Formalize the first version of **The Emotional Language Dictionary**.

---

## **Year 3: Trust, Performance & Security**

*   **Theme:** Solidify the platform as a stable, secure, and trustworthy environment.
*   **Team:** Expand core engineering to include specialists in security and infrastructure.
*   **Core Activities:**
    1.  **Technical Optimization:** Aggressively optimize the WebXR experience to be flawless on a wider range of devices ("Device-adaptive quality"). Implement the LOD system and other performance patterns.
    2.  **Third-Party Audits:** Commission and publish a full, independent security and privacy audit of the entire system.
    3.  **Data Governance Blueprint:** Formalize the **Institutional Data Governance Blueprint**, treating user data with the same gravity as a Swiss bank.
*   **Key Milestones:**
    *   **Q2:** Stable V1.0 of the URAI experience is released to the wider (but still niche) audience of reflective individuals.
    *   **Q3:** The IRB & Research Ethics Protocol is officially submitted for review.
    *   **EOY:** URAI is established as a Public Benefit Corporation (PBC) or a similar legal entity to cement its mission.

---

## **Year 4: Seeding Pattern Literacy**

*   **Theme:** Begin the careful, deliberate transition from a private tool to a shared vocabulary within trusted communities.
*   **Team:** Hire a Head of Research & Community, responsible for engaging with academic and therapeutic institutions, not mass-market consumers.
*   **Core Activities:**
    1.  **Secure Research Enclave:** Build a separate, highly secure, IRB-governed enclave where users can *explicitly opt-in* to contribute their anonymized data for academic research. This system only *listens* to URAI, honoring the `EXECUTOR_GUIDANCE.md`.
    2.  **Academic Whitepaper:** Draft the **First Academic Whitepaper** on the Archetype Evolution Engine, its mathematical model, and findings from the research enclave.
    3.  **Develop Educational Materials:** Create non-public materials for therapists to use when discussing URAI with clients who have consented to use it as part of their practice.
*   **Key Milestones:**
    *   **Q3:** First academic whitepaper is published in a reputable journal.
    *   **EOY:** The language of "seasons" and "arcs" begins to appear organically in discussions within the therapy and research communities.

---

## **Year 5: Resilience & Succession**

*   **Theme:** Ensure the project's technical, financial, and organizational resilience for the next 20 years.
*   **Team:** Formalize a board of directors for the PBC, including the Clinical Advisor and other external ethics and technology experts.
*   **Core Activities:**
    1.  **Legal & IP Fortress:** Trademark key terms and visual language to prevent misinterpretation and co-opting by third parties. Open-source certain components of the engine to foster trust.
    2.  **Succession & Governance Plan:** Draft and ratify the formal **Succession & Governance Plan**. This document ensures the project can continue and maintain its integrity even without the founder's direct involvement.
    3.  **Financial Sustainability:** Secure long-term funding from mission-aligned foundations and research grants, rather than venture capital, to avoid growth-at-all-costs pressure.
*   **Key Milestones:**
    *   **Q2:** The Succession Plan is ratified by the board.
    *   **Q4:** The "Personal Reflection Era" is declared a success. URAI is a small, stable, and deeply trusted institution. It is ready for the 20-year journey ahead.
